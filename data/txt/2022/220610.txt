Resolution proclaiming May 29 through , as the Queer and Transgender
Asian and Pacific Islander Week in the City and County of San Francisco.
WHEREAS, The Bay Area Queer and Transgender Asian and Pacific Islander (QTAPI)
Coalition was formed in , to build community, facilitate intergenerational
transfer of knowledge, and recognize the QTAPI community's historical context and
interdependence, and celebrate their unique cultural heritage and identity; and
WHEREAS, The Bay Area QTAPI Coalition includes organizations and community
groups such as Asian Pacific Islander Queer Women & Transgender Community (APIQWTC),
GLBTQ+ Asian Pacific Alliance (GAPA), Parivar Bay Area, Prism Foundation, Red Envelope
Giving Circle, API Transmasculine Anthology, GLBT Historical Society, Lavender Phoenix
(formerly API Equality Northern California (APIENC)), San Francisco Community Health
Center, San Francisco AIDS Foundation, Essence of Mana, North County Outreach, RIZE SF,
and members of the Rice Rockettes; and
WHEREAS, QTAPI people continue to experience discrimination and violence and
have been disproportionately harmed by unjust systems; and
WHEREAS, Community-based organizations across the QTAPI spectrum have
provided support, advocacy, and health and safety services to the Bay Area QTAPI
community for over 45 years; and
WHEREAS, Newer grassroots organizations formed at the turn of the millennium have
continued to respond to ongoing systems of oppression by supporting and organizing QTAPI
people in the Bay Area; and
WHEREAS, QTAPI Week is a time when organizers demonstrate the strength and
resilience of the Asian American community in the face of continued violence as well as pride
in identity as part of the LGBTQ+ community; and
WHEREAS, During QTAPI Week, the Bay Area QTAPI Coalition will host networking
activities, capacity building events, and supportive facilitated conversations that further the
work of the QTAPI movement in San Francisco and the San Francisco Bay Area; and
WHEREAS, The Bay Area QTAPI Coalition continues to grow and respond to the
issues facing the QTAPI community; now, therefore, be it
RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors hereby proclaim May 29 through , as Queer and Transgender Asian and Pacific Islander (QTAPI) Week in San Francisco;
and be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the City and County of San Francisco commends the
Bay Area QTAPI Coalition for organizing, mobilizing, and inspiring our QTAPI community, and
for elevating the numerous contributions made by the QTAPI community, locally, nationally,
and globally.
