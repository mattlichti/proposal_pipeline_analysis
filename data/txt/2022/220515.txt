Resolution approving the second amendment to the grant agreement between Five
Keys Schools and Programs and the Department of Homelessness and Supportive
Housing (HSH) to provide emergency shelter operations and services at Next Door
Shelter; increasing the grant amount by $22, 736,236 for a total amount of $32,449, 102;
and extending the grant agreement term by 36 months, from , for a total
term of , through , with five one-year options to extend
at the City's sole and absolute discretion; and authorizing HSH to enter into
amendments or modifications to the second amendment prior to its final execution by
all parties that do not materially increase the obligations or liabilities to the City and are
necessary to effectuate the purposes of the grant.
WHEREAS, The Department of Homelessness and Supportive Housing's ("HSH")
mission is to prevent homelessness when possible and to make homelessness a rare, brief,
and one-time experience in the City and County of San Francisco ("the City") through the
provision of coordinated, compassionate, and high-quality services; and
WHEREAS, With the enactment of in October 2018, the Board
of Supervisors and Mayor London Breed declared a shelter crisis and affirmed San
Francisco's commitment to a continuum of shelter and service options for people experiencing
homelessness; and
WHEREAS, As of the 2019 Point-in-Time Count, there were approximately 8,000
people experiencing homelessness in San Francisco on any given night, 64% of whom were
unsheltered; and
WHEREAS, As part of the Mayor's Homelessness Recovery Plan that was announced
in June 2020, the City is committed to maintaining and expanding shelter services; and
WHEREAS, In 2020, HSH selected Five Keys Schools and Programs ("Five Keys")
through RFQ #130 to provide emergency shelter services at Next Door Shelter located at
1001 Polk Street for up to 334 adult guests or the adjusted COVID-informed capacity based
on public health guidance; and
WHEREAS, HSH executed a grant agreement ("Agreement") with Five Keys for a total
grant amount not to exceed $9,712,866 and a total term of , through , a copy of which is on file with the Clerk of the Board of Supervisor in ; and
WHEREAS, HSH executed a no-cost first amendment to the Agreement in 2022 to
update the terms and extend the Agreement for an additional three months, for a total term of
, through , a copy of which is on file with the Clerk of the
Board of Supervisors in ; and
WHEREAS, The proposed second amendment ("Amendment") to the Agreement
would extend the Agreement by 36 months for Five Keys to continue to provide services until
, and increase the not to exceed amount by $22,736,236 for a total of
$32,449, 102; and
WHEREAS, This amendment requires Board of Supervisors approval under Section
9.118 of the Charter; therefore, be it
RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors hereby approves the Amendment to
increase the grant amount by $22,736,236 for a total amount not to exceed $32,449, 102 and
to extend the grant agreement term for 36 months from , for a total term of
, through , with five one-year options to extend at the City's
sole and absolute discretion; and be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors authorizes HSH to enter into
any amendments or modifications to the Amendment, prior to its final execution by all parties,
that HSH determines, in consultation with the City Attorney, are in the best interest of the City,
do not otherwise materially increase the obligations or liabilities of the City, are necessary or
advisable to effectuate the purposes of the grant, and are in compliance with all applicable
laws; and, beit
FURTHER RESOLVED, That within 30 days of the Amendment being executed by all
parties, HSH shall submit to the Clerk of the Board of Supervisors a completely executed copy
for inclusion in ; this requirement and obligation resides with HSH, and is for
1O the purposes of having a complete file only, and in no manner affects the validity of the
approved agreement.
