WHEREAS, On , the Airport Director approved Modification No. 1 to the
Contract to amend the Scope of Services to include onsite COVID-19 testing for Airport

licensees and other occupants of the Airport; and
WHEREAS, On , by , the Airport Commission

WHEREAS, On , by , the Airport Commission

the Clinic's operating hours and increase the Contract amount by $1,600,000 for a new

entered into by a department, board or commission requiring anticipated expenditures by the
City and County of ten million dollars, shall be subject to approval by the Board of Supervisors
by resolution; and
WHEREAS, Copies of Modification No. 3 are on file with the Clerk of the Board of
Supervisors in ; now, therefore, be it
RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors hereby approves Modification No. 3 to
Contract No. 50118.02 with Dignity Health dba St. Mary's Medical Center to exercise the 2-
year option to extend the term through , and increase the contract amount by
$1,600,000 for a new contract amount not to exceed $11,000,000; and, be it
