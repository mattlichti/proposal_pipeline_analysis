Resolution retroactively authorizing the Recreation and Park Department to accept and
expend grant funding in the amount of $4,800,000 from the California Department of
Parks and Recreation for the Buchanan Mall; approving the grant contract for the
period of , through ; and to authorize the Recreation and Park
Department to enter into amendments or modifications to the Grant Contract and to
execute further agreements that do not materially increase the obligations or liabilities
of the City and are necessary to effectuate the purposes of the Project or this
Resolution.
WHEREAS, The City, through Recreation and Park Department ("RPO"), operates and
maintains Buchanan Street Mall ("the Property"), a five-block pedestrian mall between Eddy
Street and Grove Street that includes five consecutive blocks of green space, three
playgrounds, a half basketball court, and asphalt paths; and
WHEREAS, The RPO worked closely with the Trust for Public Land (TPL), Citizen Film,
Green Streets, The Exploratorium, and the community to develop the Buchanan Mall Vision
Statement and to implement a temporary activation project on the Property; and
WHEREAS, Throughout this planning process, RPO, TPL and other project partners
have been actively seeking grant funding for park improvements consistent with the
Mall Vision; and
WHEREAS, The California State Legislature appropriated funding in the amount of
$4,800,000 to the McAllister to Fulton Block of the Buchanan Mall Project in the City and
County of San Francisco; and
WHEREAS, The California Department of Parks and Recreation ("Department") has
been delegated the responsibility by the Legislature for the administration of the funding
through a Specified Grant ("Grant"); and
WHEREAS, Said procedures established by the Department require the applicant's
• Governing Body enter into a Grant Contract; and
WHEREAS, The Grant Contract is effective from , through ;
and
WHEREAS, , the San Francisco Recreation and Park Commission
adopted approving the Recreation and Park Department's ("RPD's")
application for Specified Grant funds, authorizing the RPO General Manager to negotiate
agreements with the State concerning the administration of the grant, and recommending that
the Board of Supervisors approve the grant contract and authorize RPO to accept and expend
the grant; and
WHEREAS, As a condition of receiving the Grant, RPO is required to agree to the
terms of the attached Grant Contract substantially in the form as the draft contract which is on
file with the Clerk of the Board under and which is hereby declared to be part
of this Resolution as if set forth fully herein; and
WHEREAS, The Grant terms prohibit including indirect costs in the grant budget; and
WHEREAS, The Grant will not require an amendment to the Annual Salary Ordinance;
now, therefore, be it
RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors retroactively authorizes the Recreation
Park Department to accept and expend the grant; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors hereby waives inclusion of
indirect costs as part of this Grant budget; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors approves the Grant Contract
for the period of , through ; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors authorizes the RPO General
Manager to enter into any modifications and amendments to the Grant Contract, including to
any of its exhibits, and authorizes the RPO General Manager to execute further agreements
related to the Project, that the RPO General Manager determines, in consultation with the City
Attorney, are in the best interests of the City and do not materially increase the obligations or
liabilities of the City, are necessary or advisable to effectuate the purposes of the Project or
this Resolution, and are in compliance with all applicable laws, including the City's Charter;
and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That within 30 days of the Grant Contract being fully-executed
by all parties, RPO shall provide the final Grant Contract to the Clerk of the Board for inclusion
into the official file.
