Resolution authorizing the Recreation and Park Department to accept and expend cash
and/or in-kind grants from the San Francisco Parks Alliance (SFPA) valued at up
to $400,000 for restoration projects in the Japanese Tea Garden project for the project
term of upon approval of Board of Supervisors until Notice of Substantial Completion.
WHEREAS, The City, through the Recreation and Park Department (RPO), operates
and maintains The Japanese Tea Garden in Golden Gate Park (Garden), located in
Supervisorial District 1; and
WHEREAS, The Friends of the Japanese Tea Garden, in partnership with the RPO,
launched the Japanese Tea Garden Revitalization Campaign; the Friends of the Japanese
Tea Garden is a fiscally sponsored group of the San Francisco Parks Alliance (SFPA); the
Revitalization Campaign will focus on the restoration of the Pagoda, as well as other priority
projects which improve access to and enhance the Pagoda, including the landscaped area
around the Pagoda, the Long Bridge, and the Temple Gate; and
WHEREAS, The estimated cost for the restoration projects in the Garden is
approximately $2,000,000; the Department has budgeted a total of $1,700,000 for the
projects; the SFPA, on behalf of the Friends of the Japanese Tea Garden, has offered to raise
the remaining funds, estimated to be approximately $300,000; if there are additional costs and
if there are additional philanthropic funds, the Friends group has offered to donate up
to $400,000 (Grant); and
WHEREAS, On , the Recreation and Park Commission, by , recommended that the Board of Supervisors authorize RPO to accept and
expend the cash and/or in-kind grants; now, therefore, be it
Mayor Breed Page 1
RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors approves the Grant and authorizes the
Recreation and Park Department General Manager to accept the Grant, and to perform all
acts required of the City there under; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors authorizes the Recreation and
Park General Manager to enter into any modifications to the Grant that the Recreation and
Park General Manager determines, in consultation with the City Attorney, are in the best
interests of the City and do not materially increase the obligations or liabilities of the City, are
necessary or advisable to effectuate the purposes of the Grant or this Resolution, and are in
compliance with all applicable laws, including the City's Charter.
