Resolution authorizing the San Francisco Public Library to accept and expend a grant
in the amount of up to $1,002,647 of in-kind gifts, services, and cash monies from the
Friends of the San Francisco Public Library for direct support for a variety of public
programs and services in Fiscal Year (FY) 2022-2023.
WHEREAS, The Friends of the San Francisco Public Library (Friends) is a non-profit
organization that advocates, fundraises, and provides critical support for the San Francisco
Public Library and related literary and educational programs; and
WHEREAS, The Public Library submits proposals annually to the Friends for public
programs and services; and
WHEREAS, The types of programs and services supported by these grants include
Youth Programs, Adult Programs, Collections, Public Relations, Staff Development,
Innovation Programs; supplies, materials, furniture, fixtures, and equipment to support those
services; and
WHEREAS, Highlights of the upcoming Fiscal Year (FY) 2022-2023 program year
support include Grants to Branches, program support for children and youth services,
enhanced community outreach, and the Public Library's racial equity initiative to enhance
diversity and inclusion; and
WHEREAS, The Department proposes to maximize use of available grant funds on
program expenditures by not including indirect costs in the grant budget; now, therefore, be it
RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors hereby waives inclusion of indirect costs in
the grant budget; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Public Library is authorized to accept and expend up
to $1,002,647 of in-kind gifts, services, and cash monies awarded by the Friends of San
Francisco Public Library to support Library programs and services and related literary and
educational programs in FY2022-2023.
