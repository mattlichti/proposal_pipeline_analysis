Resolution urging Governor Gavin Newsom and the Department of Alcoholic Beverage
Control to delay implementation of the Responsible Beverage Service Training
Program.
WHEREAS, California State Assembly Bill No. 1221 (AB 1221) established the
Responsible Beverage Service (RBS) Training Program Act of 2017, and required the
Department of Alcoholic Beverage Control (ABC) to develop, implement, and administer a
curriculum for an RBS training program; and
WHEREAS, The RBS requires alcohol servers to complete a training course offered or
authorized by ABC focused on laws governing alcoholic beverages and the impact of alcohol
on the body in order to promote safer consumption of alcoholic drinks; and
WHEREAS, Due to the COVID-19 pandemic, the RBS training requirement was
delayed for a year to July 1 , 2022, with mandatory training to be completed by ;and
WHEREAS, San Francisco businesses were provided this information in May 2022,
with information only available in English and Spanish; and
WHEREAS, While other languages including Chinese, Filipino, and Vietnamese will be
available for businesses by , the delay to offer additional languages shortens the
window in which small, immigrant-owned businesses can come into compliance; and
WHEREAS, As of , approximately 22,000 alcohol servers have been
successfully certified out of an estimated one million servers in California; and
WHEREAS, ABC has approved only 35 trainers to educate the remaining hundreds of
thousands of servers, with an overwhelming majority in English and only a few in Spanish;
and
WHEREAS, Requiring businesses to be in compliance with this new program by
 is not nearly enough time to conduct outreach and education to non-native
and limited English proficient speakers; and
WHEREAS, Servers who are not in compliance with the training requirements could
subject businesses to disciplinary action, including revocation of their liquor license; and
WHEREAS, While ABC has stated that the department will take an education-first
approach to enforcement, they have not provided a timeline for when enforcement measures
will be enacted, disproportionately impacting non-native and limited English-speaking servers
and immigrant-owned small businesses; and
WHEREAS, Immigrant-owned small businesses that disproportionately struggled
through the pandemic, facing racism and prejudice, are now being faced with a tight timeline
to come into compliance with the RBS, which represents an additional barrier to recovery;
now, therefore, be it
RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors of the City and County of San Francisco
urges Governor Gavin Newsom and the Department of Alcoholic Beverage Control to delay
implementation of the Responsible Beverage Service Training Program; and, be it
Francisco urges Governor Newsom and the Department of Alcoholic Beverage Control to
create a robust outreach plan to non-native and limited English proficient servers and
businesses, and expedite hiring of non-English speaking trainers; and, be it
Supervisors Chan; Peskin, Mar. Walton
Francisco directs the Clerk of the Board to transmit this resolution to Governor Newsom and
the Department of Alcoholic Beverage Control.
