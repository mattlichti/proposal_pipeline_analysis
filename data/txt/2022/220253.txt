PREPARED IN COMMITTEE
 511212022 RESOLUTION N0.237-22

Resolution determining that the person-to-person and premises-to-premises
transfer of a Type-21 off-sale general beer, wine, and distilled spirits liquor
license to Bear Eagle, Inc., doing business as SFGIFTBASKETS.COM, located at 2
Embarcadero Center, Suite R-2105 (District 3), will serve the public convenience
or necessity of the City and County of San Francisco, in accordance with
California Business and Professions Code, Section 23958.4.
1O WHEREAS, Bear Eagle, Inc., is seeking the person-to-person and premises-to-
premises transfer of a Type-21 off-sale general beer, wine, and distilled spirits liquor
license to do business as SFGIFTBASKETS.COM, located at 2 Embarcadero Center,
Suite R-2105 (District 3); and
WHEREAS, The Planning Department has verified that the area is properly
zoned and recommends approval; and
WHEREAS, The Police Department has filed zero protests and zero letters of
support with the Department of Alcoholic Beverage Control under California Business
and Professions Code, Section 24013, and recommends that the Department of
Alcoholic Beverage Control issue the liquor license with conditions; and
WHEREAS, The Police Department is recommending approval with no
conditions; now, therefore, be it
RESOLVED, That in accordance with California Business and Professions Code,
Section 23958.4, the Board of Supervisors of the City and County of San Francisco
hereby determines that the person-to-person and premises-to-premises transfer of a
Type-21 off-sale general beer, wine, and distilled spirits liquor license to do business as
SFGIFTBASKETS.COM, located at 2 Embarcadero Center, Suite R-2105 (District 3),
will serve the public convenience or necessity of the City and County of San Francisco.
