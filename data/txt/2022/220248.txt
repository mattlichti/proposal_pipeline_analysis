Resolution changing the street name of Sonora Lane, between O'Farrell Street and
Terra Anza Avenue, in the Anza Vista neighborhood, to "Vicha Ratanapakdee Way."
WHEREAS, On , the Board of Supervisors adopted , declaring the Board's intent to rename Sonora Lane, between O'Farrell Street and
Terra Anza Avenue, to "Vicha Ratanapakdee Way," and directing the Department of Public
Works to initiate the City's street naming procedures; and
1O WHEREAS, Sonora Lane is the existing name of the public right-of-way between
O'Farrell Street and Terra Anza Avenue, and runs parallel to Anza Vista and St. Joseph's
Avenues, and is perpendicular to O'Farrell Street and Terra Anza Avenue; and
WHEREAS, Sonora Lane is in the Anza Vista neighborhood and near what was the
home of Mr. Vicha Ratanapakdee, and which remains the home of his family; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Ratanapakdee was an 84-year-old immigrant from Thailand who was
well known in the Anza Vista neighborhood for his hour-long walks each morning, a ritual that
kept him vibrant and healthy during the COVID-19 pandemic; and
WHEREAS, On , Mr. Ratanapakdee, while walking peacefully in the
neighborhood, was suddenly and violently shoved to the pavement by an assailant, resulting
in severe head injuries from which Mr. Ratanapakdee tragically died shortly after the attack;
and
WHEREAS, Stop AAPI Hate, a California-based coalition, has recorded nearly 7,000
hate incidents involving Asian Americans and Pacific Islanders nationwide since the beginning
of the COVID-19 pandemic; and
WHEREAS, Since Mr. Ratanapakdee's passing, his daughter has dedicated herself to
working to ensure survivors of these attacks and their families are supported through city
services, law enforcement, and the justice system; and
WHEREAS, Mr. Ratanapakdee's daughter has also worked to amplify the voices of
Asian Americans and the Thai and Southeast Asian communities through her participation in
marches, rallies, and many #stopAAPIHate events; and
WHEREAS, Renaming Sonora Lane to "Vicha Ratanapakdee Lane" would
commemorate and memorialize Mr. Ratanapakdee's death as a symbol of a movement
towards equality, anti-discrimination, and solidarity with the Asian American and Pacific
1O Islander Community, and as a reminder of the importance of peace among all peoples, in
accordance with Mr. Ratanapakdee's deepest values; and
WHEREAS, The Department of Public Works conducted a review and investigation of
the proposed street name change as directed under , finding that the
proposed street name does not exist in the City's current list of official street names, and
therefore does not warrant objection under Public Works Code, Section 423, and further
finding that, pursuant to the process for changing street names set forth in the "Establishing
Street Names" bulletin posted on the Department's webpage, no City Agencies or fronting
property owners object to the proposed name change; and
WHEREAS, On , the Department of Public Works notified the Clerk of
the Board of Supervisors that it had completed its investigation into the proposed renaming of
Sonora Lane, the only segment of which runs between Terra Vista Avenue and O'Farrell
Street, and the results of which investigation are on file with the Clerk of the Board in , the file for this Resolution; and
WHEREAS, In compliance with the requirement of California Streets and Highways
Code, Section 970.5, notice of a public hearing was posted at least 10 days prior to the
hearing on this Resolution before the Land Use and Transportation Committee, in at least
three public places along the street proposed to be affected by the renaming; now, therefore,
be it
RESOLVED, That pursuant to its authority under California Government Code, Section
34091.1 and California Streets and Highways Code, Sections 970.5 and 5026, the Board of
Supervisors hereby renames Sonora Lane, between Terra Vista Avenue and O'Farrell Street,
to "Vicha Ratanapakdee Way" to honor Mr. Ratanapakdee's legacy, and to make clear that
the City and County of San Francisco stands with its Asian American Pacific Islander
communities during this extraordinarily difficult time for those communities; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors directs the Municipal
Transportation Agency to erect street signs in accordance with Transportation Code, Section
2.2, which signs should indicate both the new and the former names of the street affected by
this Resolution-the new street name, "Vicha Ratanapakdee Way," in large letters and the
former name, "Sonora Lane," in smaller letters above or beneath the new name-and such
signs indicating the two street names shall remain posted for five years and then replaced
with signs indicating only the new street name "Vicha Ratanapakdee Way;" and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That in adopting this legislation, the Board of Supervisors
does not intend to effectuate any change in City department jurisdictional control over the
public right-of-way to be renamed as "Vicha Ratanapakdee Way;" and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors directs the Clerk of the Board
to promptly forward a copy of this Resolution to the County Surveyor in accordance with
California Streets and Highways Code, Section 5026, and that the County Surveyor include
the new street name on the Official Map of the City; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors directs the Department of
Public Works to add "Vicha Ratanapakdee Way" to the City's official list of street names.
