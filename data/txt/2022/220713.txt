Resolution urging airline service contractors operating at San Francisco International
Airport to protect workers and to create and maintain as many full-time jobs as the
flight schedules will allow.
WHEREAS, The San Francisco Board of Supervisors acted during the COVID-19
pandemic to ensure that low wage, immigrant, and essential workers of color were protected,
passing critical pieces of legislation, including the Healthy Airport Ordinance, to ensure that
airport workers had access to fully paid family healthcare; and
WHEREAS, Airport workers faced months of unemployment and dangerous working
conditions during the pandemic; and
WHEREAS, The San Francisco Board of Supervisors has worked to ensure that these
most impacted communities are prioritized in the economic recovery; and
WHEREAS, With a contracted airline service workforce made up largely of Black,
Brown and immigrant service workers, this industry's recovery presents both racial justice and
economic justice concerns; and
WHEREAS, The domestic recovery of the airline is well underway with national travel
activity already in excess of 90% of pre-pandemic levels; and
WHEREAS, Airline contractors received billions in CARES Act bailout money in
addition to over $50 billion given to their client airlines, and the airline industry prior to COVID
was coming off of its most successful decades ever and five of the most profitable years in
industry history; and
Supervisors Mandelman; Walton, Mar. Preston, Chan, Ronen, Safai
WHEREAS, Contracted out passenger service workers at SFO report that despite
increased business at the airport, many are working part-time schedules while experiencing
increased workload and unsafe staffing levels; and
WHEREAS, Airline employers cutting worker's hours undermines San Francisco's
living wage laws and airport safety standards, pushing experienced workers out of the
industry; now, therefore, be it
RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors of the City and County of San Francisco
hereby urges airline service contractors operating at San Francisco International Airport and
their airline clients to create and maintain as many full-time jobs as the flight schedule will
allow; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors hereby urges the airline service
contractors to settle a fair union contract with SEIU USWW that protects full-time work and
provides for good family sustaining jobs.
Supervisors Mandelman; Walton. Mar, Preston, Chan, Ronen, Safai
