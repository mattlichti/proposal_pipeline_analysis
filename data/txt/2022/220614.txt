Resolution urging the Controller to submit an application to the Refuse Rate Board to
adjust the rates for residential refuse collection pursuant to the Refuse Collection and
Disposal Ordinance.
WHEREAS, The City's Refuse Rate Board sets the rates for the collection and disposal
of residential refuse pursuant to the Refuse Collection and Disposal Ordinance (the "Refuse
Ordinance"), and in 2017, ordered a significant rate increase; and
1O WHEREAS, Starting in 2020, a series of public reports revealed that the City's refuse
collection system was in need of reform, as evidenced by Recology, the City's sole permitted
refuse collector, overcharging San Francisco residents and businesses by almost $100
million; the City corrected this error, but even afterwards continued to have significant
concerns that Recology's expenses may have been inflated and had difficulty ascertaining
answers because of the lack of transparency in the current regulatory structure; and
WHEREAS, On , the City Controller released a public report describing
errors and inconsistencies in Recology's 2017 rate application, which had been used to justify
the 2017 rate increases; and
WHEREAS, The 2017 rates are still in effect, along with provisions that allow those
rates to increase each year based on the formula that the Rate Board approved at the time,
and the rates will continue to remain effect until such time as the Refuse Rate Board adjusts
the rate; and
WHEREAS, The Refuse Ordinance allows any person, not]ust Recology, to submit an
application to the Refuse Rate Board to adjust the rates; and
WHEREAS, The Controller has significant expertise and knowledge as to the refuse
rate-setting process and would be well-qualified to submit an application to the Refuse Rate
Board to adjust the rates; and
WHEREAS, The voters at the , election will consider Proposition F, which,
if adopted, would require the Controller to serve as Refuse Rate Administrator and monitor
rates and recommend adjustments to the Rate Board, and would appoint a Ratepayer
Representative to replace the Controller on the Refuse Rate Board; and
WHEREAS, The rates should be just and reasonable, and it essential to conduct the
rate adjustment process at the highest standards of fairness and transparency; now,
1O therefore, be it
RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors urges the Controller to submit an
application to the Refuse Rate Board to adjust the refuse rates.
