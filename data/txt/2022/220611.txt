Resolution urging the California Assembly to pass California State Senate Bill No. 793,
introduced by California Senator Scott Wiener on , authorizing the
Department of Alcoholic Beverage Control to issue a music venue license, and
allowing cities and counties to create "entertainment zones," within which on-sale
licensees can permit consumers to leave the premises with open containers of
alcoholic beverages.
. 10 WHEREAS, The Alcoholic Beverage Control Act contains various provisions regulating
the application for, issuance of, suspension of, and conditions that may be imposed upon
alcoholic beverage licenses by the Department of Alcoholic Beverage Control; and
WHEREAS, Existing law provides for various annual fees for the issuance of alcoholic
beverage licenses, depending upon the type of license issued; and
WHEREAS, California State Senate Bill No. (SB) 793 would authorize the Department
to issue a music venue license that would allow the licensee to sell beer, wine, and distilled
spirits at retail for consumption on the premises in a music entertainment facility; and
WHEREAS, Under existing law, any person possessing an open container of an
alcoholic beverage in any public place owned by a city, county, or city and county, or any
regional park or recreation and park district, is guilty of an infraction if the jurisdiction has
enacted an ordinance that prohibits the possession of those containers or the consumption of
alcoholic beverages in those areas, except as specified and
WHEREAS, Existing law authorizes the legislative body of any city, county or city and
county to adopt ordinances regulating zoning within its jurisdiction; and
WHEREAS, Existing law authorizes a licensed beer manufacturer, a licensed
winegrower, and any on-sale licensee to sell certain alcoholic beverages for consumption on
or off the premises, as specified; and
WHEREAS, SB 793 would authorize a licensed beer manufacturer, a licensed
winegrower, and any on-sale licensee to permit consumers to leave the premises with open
containers of alcoholic beverages for consumption off the premises within an entertainment
zone, subject to certain conditions; and
WHEREAS, SB 793 would define "entertainment zone" for purposes of the Alcoholic
Beverage Control Act as a zone created by a city, county, or city and county ordinance on or
after , that authorizes consumption of one or more types of alcoholic
beverages on public streets, sidewalks, or public rights-of-way in that zone; and
WHEREAS, On , SB 793 passed the Senate with a unanimous 32-0
vote; now, therefore, be it
RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors supports California Senate Bill No. 793;
and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors hereby directs the Clerk of the
Board to transmit a copy of this Resolution to San Francisco's state legislative delegation, the
Office of the Chief Clerk of the Assembly, and the Office of Governor Newsom.
