Resolution retroactively authorizing the Department of Public Health to accept and
expend a grant increase from the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention for
participation in a program, entitled "San Francisco Department of Public Health Climate
and Health Program actions to reduce the disproportionate impacts of climate change,"
in the amount of $86,287 for a total amount of $300,000 for the period of , through .
WHEREAS, The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) has agreed to
fund the Department of Public Health (DPH) in the amount of $300,000 for participation in a
program, entitled "San Francisco Department of Public Health Climate and Health Program
actions to reduce the disproportionate impacts of climate change," for the period of , through ; and
WHEREAS, This funding opportunity is to enhance the resilience of San Francisco to
the health impacts of climate change; and
WHEREAS, This funding will allow the climate and health program to pursue
collaboration with stakeholders to create a Climate Impact Compendium that includes data on
local climate projections, health impacts, social determinates of health, and adaptative
capacity; manage the Citywide Heat and Air Quality Resilience Project (HAQR) to identify
medium-to-long-term strategies to become resilient to extreme heat and wildfire smoke
events; and create evaluation tools to understand effectiveness of current and future climate
resilience strategies; and
WHEREAS, The grant does not require an Annual Salary Ordinance Amendment; and
WHEREAS, A grant increase of $86,287 from $213,713 was approved for the period
of , through ; and
WHEREAS, A request for retroactive approval is being sought because DPH received
the award notice on , for a project start date of ; and
WHEREAS, The grant budget includes a provision for indirect costs in the amount of
$51,761; now, therefore, be it
RESOLVED, That DPH is hereby authorized to retroactively accept and expend a grant
in the amount of $300,000 from the CDC; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That DPH is hereby authorized to retroactively accept and
expend the grant funds pursuant to Administrative Code, Section 10.170-1.
