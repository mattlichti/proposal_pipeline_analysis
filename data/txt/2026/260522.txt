Resolution urging State officials to support California State Senate Bill No. 436
(Wahab), the "Keeping Californians Housed Act," which seeks to prevent displacement
by extending the notice period for renters to pay or vacate from three days to 14 days,
bringing California in line with other states.
WHEREAS, Approximately 65% of Californians are considered rent-burdened, paying
more than 30% of their income on rent; and
WHEREAS, Many Californians are one late or missed paycheck, housing voucher, or
fixed-income payment away from eviction and homelessness; and
WHEREAS, Recent federal policies on immigration and cuts to critical federal welfare
programs have strained working-class San Franciscans' ability to pay rent; and
WHEREAS, Most California tenants only have three days after a "pay or quit" notice is
issued to pay missed rent before the eviction process begins, one of the shortest in the nation;
and
WHEREAS, Federally subsidized housing programs generally require landlords to give
tenants at least 30 days' notice before terminating a tenancy for nonpayment of rent - a
protection now under federal threat - underscoring how extreme California's three-day "pay
or quit" timeline is for unsubsidized tenants; and
WHEREAS, A report from Standford Law School shows that many California tenants
who receive rental assistance or have the ability to pay missed rent are nonetheless being
evicted, often over trivial amounts of money or due to bureaucratic delays of their rental
assistance; and
WHEREAS, Rent relief programs that dedicate public dollars to homelessness
prevention are effectively maximized by policies that allow recipients to pay past due rent to
stay housed and landlords to receive the funds; and
WHEREAS, California State Senate Bill No. 436 (Wahab), the "Keeping Californians
Housed Act", would extend the notice period for tenants from three to fourteen business days
in California; now, therefore, be it
RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors of the City and County of San Francisco
hereby urges State representatives to support California State Senate Bill No. 436 (Wahab),
the "Keeping Californians Housed Act"; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the Board shall transmit copies of this
Resolution to Assembly Member Matt Haney, Assembly Member Catherine Stefani, State
Senator Scott Weiner, State Senator Aisha Wahab, and the City's State Lobbyists.
