Resolution approving the third amendment to the grant agreement between Tenderloin
Housing Clinic and the Department of Homelessness and Supportive Housing ("HSH"),
for master lease stewardship, property management, and support services at 16
permanent supportive housing sites; extending the term by 18 months from , for a total term of , through ; increasing the
agreement amount by $61,951,806 for a new total amount not to exceed $303,609,319;

Amendment that do not materially increase the obligations or liabilities, or materially
decrease the benefits to the City and are necessary or advisable to effectuate the
purposes of the Agreement.
WHEREAS, The mission of the Department of Homelessness and Supportive Housing
("HSH" or "Department") is to prevent homelessness when possible and make homelessness
rare, brief, and one-time in the City and County of San Francisco ("the City") through the
provision of coordinated, compassionate, and high-quality services; and
WHEREAS, The nonprofit provider Tenderloin Housing Clinic, Inc. ("THC") has
extensive experience providing supportive services and property management at Permanent
Supportive Housing (PSH) sites in San Francisco; and
WHEREAS, THC is the longtime provider of supportive services and property
management at 16 master-leased PSH sites (the "Master Lease Hotels"); and
WHEREAS, The Human Services Agency ("HSA'') selected THC to provide services at
the Master Lease Hotels through Notice of Funding Opportunity #592 in 2014; and
WHEREAS, When HSH was created in 2016, the Department inherited HSA's
agreement with THC for the Master Lease Hotels; and
WHEREAS, In 2020, HSH awarded the Agreement to THC through the Department's
streamlined contracting authority for homeless services under Administrative Code,
Chapter 21.B; and
WHEREAS, In June 2020, HSH and THC entered into an Agreement for master lease
stewardship, property management, and support services at the Master Lease Hotels
("Original Agreement"); and
