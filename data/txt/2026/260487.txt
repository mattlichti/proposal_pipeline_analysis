Resolution approving and authorizing the Director of Property, on behalf of the
Department of Public Health, to execute an Office Lease for the continued use of office
space located at 796 Brannan Street, floor 2 with 796 Brannan Street, LLC, as Landlord,
effective upon approval of this Resolution by the Board of Supervisors and the Mayor
and upon execution of the Office Lease by the Director of Property, with an estimated
commencement date of , through , with two five-year
extension options, the initial term starting at a monthly base rent of $3,389.66, for a
total annual base rent of $40,675.96 with annual 3% increases; and authorizing the
Director of Property to take other actions with respect to the Office Lease, including
entering into amendments or modifications and exercising the extension options, or
taking other actions with respect to the Office Lease that the Director of Property
deems to be in the best interest of the City, and do not materially increase the
obligations or liabilities of the City, do not materially decrease the benefits of the City,
are necessary or advisable to effectuate the purposes of the Office Lease or this
Resolution, and are in compliance with all applicable laws, including the City's Charter.
WHEREAS, The Department of Public Health ("DPH, through Jail Health Services,
operates an office site on the second floor of 796 Brannan Street (the "Office") to provide
office and administrative space for medical and clinical staff in support of the delivery of
medical and behavioral health care services to individuals housed in the jails of the City and
County of San Francisco ("City"), including services associated with inmate intake and
release; and
WHEREAS, The City originally entered into a month-to-month lease commencing
, with a predecessor-in-interest to 796 Brannan Street, LLC ("Landlord"), the
current owner of the Office; and
WHEREAS, The City's Real Estate Division, in consultation with DPH and the Office of
the City Attorney, negotiated a new Office Lease Agreement ("Office Lease") with the
Landlord for the Office; and
WHEREAS, The Office Lease proposes (i) an initial five-year term with two options to
extend the term for five years each, the initial term starting at a monthly base rent of
$3,389.66, for a total annual base rent of $40,675.96 (or $41.17 per square foot), increasing
annually at 3%; (ii) with all utilities included except for trash pick-up for which the City shall
pay an additional $200 monthly without any annual increase; and (iii) the right to use two
parking stalls on the property's parking facilities, in common with other tenants, at all times. A
copy of the proposed Office Lease is on file with the Clerk of the Board in ;
and
WHEREAS, In accordance with Section 23.27 of the San Francisco Administrative
Code, an appraisal is not required because the annual base rent is less than $45 per square
foot, and the Director of Property has determined the base rent is at or below fair market
value; now, therefore, be it
RESOLVED, That, in accordance with the recommendation of the Director of the DPH,
and the Director of Property, the Board of Supervisors hereby approves the Office Lease and
the Director of Property is hereby authorized to take all actions on behalf of the City, as
tenant, to execute the Office Lease on the terms and conditions set forth herein, to enter into
any additions, amendments or other modifications (including without limitation, the exhibits) to
the Lease that the Director of Property determines, in consultation with the Director of DPH,
are in the best interests of the City, do not materially increase the obligations or liabilities of
the City, do not materially decrease the benefits to the City and are necessary or advisable to
effectuate the purposes of the Office Lease or this Resolution, and are in compliance with all
applicable laws, including City's Charter; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Office Lease contains language indemnifying and
holding harmless the Landlord from, and agreeing to defend the Landlord against any and all
claims, losses, damages, costs and expenses, including reasonable attorney's fees
(collectively, "Claims"), incurred as a result of the City's use of the Office, or any negligent
acts or omissions of the City or its agents, employees, officers, and contractors (collectively,
"Agents") in, on, or about the Office or the property; except those Claims arising out of the
negligence or willful misconduct of Landlord or its Agents; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That within 30 days of the Office Lease being fully executed
by all parties, the Director of Property shall provide the fully executed copy to the Clerk of the
Board for inclusion into the official file and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the City shall have the right to lease the Office as
described in the Office Lease, subject to the appropriation of sufficient funds to pay for the
City's lease obligations by the Board of Supervisors and Mayor in their sole and absolute
discretion, and if such funds are not appropriated in any subsequent fiscal year, the City may
terminate the Office Lease at no cost with written notice to Landlord.
