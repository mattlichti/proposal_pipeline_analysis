Resolution approving and authorizing the General Manager of the San Francisco Public
Utilities Commission to execute Amendment No. 1 to Contract No. PRO.0163(R),
Engineering Services for the Emergency Firefighting Water System and City's Water
Distribution System, with LEE + RO and AGS Joint Venture, increasing the contract
amount by $5,000,000 for a total not to exceed contract amount of $13,000,000 with no
change to the contract duration of , through , to provide
additional engineering design and support for pipeline improvement projects, pursuant
to Charter, Section 9.118.
WHEREAS, The San Francisco Public Utilities Commission (SFPUC) Water Enterprise
Local Water System Capital Improvement Program (Local Water CIP) is a 10-year plan of
scheduled projects to improve system assets and maintain level of service goals; and
WHEREAS, The SFPUC updates the Local Water CIP each year for integration with
the SFPUC's Financial Plan and rate-setting and the Local Water CIP funds improvements to
San Francisco's drinking water pipelines under the Local Water Conveyance/Distribution
System program, also known as Linear Asset Management Program (LAMP); and
WHEREAS, The Local Water CIP also funds, along with the Earthquake Safety and
Emergency Response bond series, improvements to the City's Emergency Firefighting Water
System; and
WHEREAS, LAMP replaces and renews feeder and distribution mains for the 1,230
miles of pipe in San Francisco's drinking water distribution system; and
WHEREAS, The SFPUC's goal is to replace approximately 10 to 15 miles of pipe per
year depending on funding availability and improvements include replacement, rehabilitation,
relining, seismic resiliency, and cathodic protection of all pipe size categories to extend or
renew pipeline useful life; and
WHEREAS, The City's Emergency Firefighting Water System (EFWS) is a complex
network of reservoirs, tanks, pipelines, cisterns, pump stations, fireboats and their related
manifolds to provide the San Francisco Fire Department with water availability to fight large
fires and fires following large seismic events when the domestic water system may be
impacted; and
WHEREAS, On , by , the SFPUC
Commission awarded Contract No. PRO.0163(R), Engineering Services for the Emergency
Firefighting Water System and City's Water Distribution System, to LEE+ RO and AGS Joint
Venture, for an amount not to exceed $8,000,000 and with a contract duration of eight years;
WHEREAS, The original amount of this contract was based on the project budgets
included in the 10-Year Water Enterprise Capital Plan for Fiscal Years 2019-2028 that was
approved in February 2018; and
WHEREAS, Since its initiation, this contract has primarily supported four main efforts:
1) system performance evaluation of EFWS; 2) planning for Central Pump Station; 3) planning
and design for EFWS Pipeline Contracts A and B; and 4) support for various LAMP Projects;
WHEREAS, During the planning and design phases of the EFWS projects, several
factors necessitated the budget increase, including a higher seismic level of service,
alignment changes to improve system robustness, unforeseen geotechnical complexities, and
evaluations for potential cost reductions due to market conditions; and
WHEREAS, The updated forecasted pre-construction and construction costs are
reflected in the most recently adopted 10-Year Capital Improvement Plan for Fiscal Years
2027-2036; and
WHEREAS, The SFPUC is seeking approval of Amendment No.1 to Contract
PRO.0163(R), to provide additional contracting capacity for engineering support services to
complete the planning and final design for EFWS Pipeline contracts A through C, and to
support ongoing LAMP projects; and
WHEREAS, The contract work provided by the consultant is part of the planning and
engineering services under the direction of the SFPUC Engineering Management Bureau; and
WHEREAS, Funds will come from individual projects, either within the Water Enterprise
Local Water Capital Improvement Program and/or Earthquake Safety and Emergency
Response bonds, for Emergency Firefighting Water System projects; and
WHEREAS, The Contract Monitoring Division (CMD) established a 10% Local
Business Enterprise (LBE) subcontracting requirement for this contract and the contractor
remains committed to a 10% LBE subcontractor participation; and
WHEREAS, This action does not constitute a project under the California
Environmental Quality Act (CEQA) Guidelines, Section 15378, because work will consist of
engineering design and support services only, and no support services during construction
and closeout will be initiated until any necessary environmental review has been completed as
required by the CEQA and the SFPUC Commission has reviewed and considered the CEQA
determination and approved the projects; and
WHEREAS, Charter, Section 9.11 S(b), requires Board of Supervisors' approval by
Resolution of any contract which, when entered into, extends over 10 years, and of any
contract which, when entered into, costs the City $10,000,000 or more; and
WHEREAS, The Original Agreement is on file with the Clerk of the Board of
Supervisors in , which is hereby declared to be a part of this Resolution as if
set forth fully herein; and
WHEREAS, The Amendment is consistent with the Civil Service Commission's
approval obtained on , under Personal Service Contract No. DHRPSC0006067;
WHEREAS, On , by , the SFPUC Commission
authorized the General Manager of the SF PUC to execute Amendment No. 1 to Contract No.
PRO.0163(R), Engineering Services for the Emergency Firefighting Water System and City's
Water Distribution System, with LEE+ RO and AGS Joint Venture, increasing the contract
amount by $5,000,000 for a total not to exceed contract amount of $13,000,000 with no
change to the contract duration of , through , to provide additional
engineering design and support for pipeline improvement projects, subject to Board of
Supervisors' approval pursuant to Charter, Section 9.118; now, therefore, be it
RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors hereby approves the Amendment in
substantially the form contained in ; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors hereby authorizes the General
Manager of the SFPUC to execute Amendment No. 1 to Contract No. PRO.0163(R) with LEE
+ RO and AGS Joint Venture, increasing the contract amount by $5,000,000 for a total not to
exceed contract amount of $13,000,000 with no change to the contract duration; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors authorizes SFPUC to make any
modifications to the Amendment, prior to its final execution by all parties, that the SFPUC
determines, in consultation with the City Attorney, are consistent with this Resolution, in the
best interest of the City, do not materially increase the obligations or liabilities of the City, are
necessary or advisable to effectuate the purposes of the Amendment, and are in compliance
with all applicable laws, including City's Charter; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That within 30 days of Amendment No. 1 being fully executed
by all parties, the SFPUC shall provide the signed revised contracts to the Clerk of the Board
for inclusion in the official file; this requirement and obligation reside with the SFPUC and is
for purposes of having a complete file only, and in no manner affects the validity of approved
Amendment.
