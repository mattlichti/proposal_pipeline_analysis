Resolution supporting California State Assembly Bill No. 1837, introduced by
Assembly Member Mark Gonzalez, which will authorize the use of video imaging to
enforce parking and stopping violations in transit-only lanes and bikeways indefinitely.
WHEREAS, In 2025, San Francisco ranked as the third most congested city in the
United States with most personal vehicle commuters losing over 116 hours a year to traffic
and almost 100 traffic related fatalities since 2023, including individuals who were killed while
walking, cycling, or driving; and
WHEREAS, As revitalization of San Francisco's economic centers continues to
increase, so does foot, bike, and vehicle traffic, highlighting the need for a proactive approach
to improving the city's roads and bikeways; and
WHEREAS, Double parking exacerbates existing cumbersome traffic conditions within
San Francisco, such as by raising travel times, reducing road capacity, increasing congestion,
and slowing traffic speeds; and
WHEREAS, Moreover, double parking reduces the overall safety of streets and
bikeways by forcing bicyclists and other vehicles to weave into traffic at high speeds; and
WHEREAS, Since the adoption of Transit-First Policy in 1973, San Francisco has
committed itself to improving public transit and street safety through programs such as Mayor
Lurie's Street Safety Directive and Vision Zero, our city's goal to achieve zero traffic related
fatalities annually; and
WHEREAS, Assembly Bill No. 361 (Ward, 2023) authorized designated law
enforcement or government employees to review video images of committed traffic violations
from city-owned public vehicles and issue a notice of violation within 15 calendar days to
offenders; and
WHEREAS, From 2025, citywide citations for parking violations issued in bike lanes
has decreased dramatically from over 1000 monthly to less than 300 per month, indicating
necessity of enforcement; and
WHEREAS, Assembly Bill No. 1837 (AB 1837) would extend the authorization for the
use of video imaging to enforce parking and stopping violations indefinitely, building upon the
successes of Assembly Bill No. 361 (2023); and
WHEREAS, AB 1837 would additionally expand the use of video imaging on city-
owned public transit vehicles for the enforcement of stopping and parking violations in
bikeways; now, therefore, be it
RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors of the City and County of San Francisco
supports AB 1837 (Gonzalez) as a means of improving the effectiveness of public transit in
San Francisco, improving citywide traffic conditions, and furthering transit-first policy; and, be
it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors encourages timely adoption
and implementation of AB 1837 to further affirm their commitment to safer streets and
bikeways for all San Franciscans; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Clerk of the Board of Supervisors shall transmit a
copy of this resolution upon passage to the Governor of California, the President pro Tempore
of the California State Senate, the Speaker of the California Assembly, Assembly Member
Mark Gonzalez, and the offices of San Francisco's legislative delegation.
