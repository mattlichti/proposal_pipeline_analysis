Resolution retroactively authorizing the Office of Cannabis to accept and expend a
grant award in the not to exceed amount of $2,169,952 and to expend interest earned or
accrued on grant funds for the grant term of , through ,
from the Governor's Office of Business and Economic Development for the Cannabis
Equity Grants Program for Local Jurisdictions; to execute the agreement with the
Governor's Office of Business and Economic Development, and any extensions or
amendments thereto, on behalf of the City and County of San Francisco; to indemnify
the Governor's Office of Business and Economic Development for liability arising out
of the performance of this contract; and approving the grant agreement pursuant to
Charter, Section 9.11 B(a).
WHEREAS, The members of the California Legislature have recognized the need for
equity grant funding; and
WHEREAS, Funding has been provided to the Governor's Office of Business and
Economic Development ("GO-Biz") to provide grant funds to local governments pursuant to
the Business and Professions Code, Section 26240; and
WHEREAS, The San Francisco Office of Cannabis ("Office of Cannabis") operates a
local equity program for commercial cannabis activity, created by Ordinance No. 230-17,
approved , effective ; and
WHEREAS, In December 2025, the Office of Cannabis submitted to GO-Biz an
application for a grant from the Local Equity Grant Funding program to support the San
Francisco Cannabis Equity Program and Equity Program participants; and
WHEREAS, In January 2026, GO-Biz awarded the Office of Cannabis a grant of
$2,169,952 in funds to support its Cannabis Equity Program; and
WHEREAS, The grant budget includes provision for indirect costs of $212,083.1 0; and
WHEREAS, The GO-Biz grant does not require an Annual Salary Ordinance
amendment; and
WHEREAS, Matching funds are in the amount of $1,419,952 and are sourced from
eligible personnel costs and equity contributions; and
WHEREAS, The Office of Cannabis intends to use GO-Biz grant funds, including any
positive interest accrued on the grant funds, to assist local equity applicants and permittees
through its local equity program as described in its application for grant funds; now, therefore,
RESOLVED, That the Office of Cannabis is hereby authorized under Administrative
Code, Section 10.170-1, to accept and expend up to $2,169,952 of GO-Biz funds to assist
local equity applicants through its local equity program for commercial cannabis activity; and,
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors hereby retroactively approves
under Charter, Section 9.118, the grant agreement with GO-Biz included in the official file with
a total not to exceed amount of $2,169,952 for a grant term of , through , for grants to benefit equity cannabis businesses; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors hereby approves inclusion of
indirect costs in the amount of $212,083.10 in the grant budget; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors hereby authorizes the Deputy
City Administrator to execute by electronic signature on behalf of the Office of Cannabis and
the City and County of San Francisco the grant agreement included in the official file, and any
extensions or amendments to the grant agreement; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That any liability arising out of the performance of this
contract, including civil court actions for damages, shall be the responsibility of the grant
recipient and the authorizing agency; GO-Biz and the State of California disclaim
responsibility for any such liability; and be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That within thirty (30) days of the Grant Agreement being fully
executed by all parties, the Director of the Office of Cannabis shall provide a copy to the Clerk
of the Board of Supervisors for inclusion in the official file.
