Resolution approving Amendment No. 2 to the agreement between the City and County
of San Francisco, acting by and through, the Department of Public Health (DPH), and
Richmond Area Multi Services, Inc., to provide peer-to-peer employment and peer
specialist mental health certificate services, to extend the term by three years from
, for a new term of , through , and to increase
the amount by $19,095,330 for a new total not to exceed amount of $48,211,511; and to

materially increase the obligations or liabilities to the City and are necessary to
effectuate the purposes of the agreement or this Resolution.
WHEREAS, On , , and , respectively,
the Department of Public Health (DPH) issued a Request for Qualifications for peer-to-peer
employment and peer specialist mental health certificate programs (RFQ 27-2020), for Mental
Health Services Act - intensive case management/full service partnership to outpatient
transition support (RFQ 22-2018), and for Mental Health Services Act - peer-to-peer
behavioral health services (RFQ 43-2019); and
WHEREAS, Richmond Area Multi Services, Inc. submitted a proposal and was the
highest ranked proposer; and
WHEREAS, DPH awarded the contract to Richmond Area Multi Services, Inc.; and
WHEREAS, The contract is consistent with the Civil Service Commission's approval
obtained on , , and , respectively, under
Personal Service Contract Nos. 46266-14/15, 40587-17/18, and 49279-17/18; and
WHEREAS, On , DPH and Richmond Area Multi Services, Inc. entered into
an agreement to provide peer-to-peer employment and peer specialist mental health
certificate services ("Original Agreement"); and
WHEREAS, The Original Agreement has a term of , through , and a not to exceed amount of $9,862,750; and
WHEREAS, DPH amended the Original Agreement on , to extend
the term to , and to increase the maximum expenditure by $19,253,431 (the
"First Amendment"); and
WHEREAS, DPH wishes to amend the agreement by extending the term to June 30,
1O 2029, and increasing the maximum expenditure by $19,095,330 to $48,211,511 (the "Second
Amendment"); and
WHEREAS, The Second Amendment is consistent with the Civil Service Commission's
approval, to be considered on , under Personal Service Contract No.
DHRPSC0001895, and with the Department of Human Resources on behalf of the Civil
Service Commission's approval obtained on , under Personal Service
Contract No. DHRPSC0005396; and
WHEREAS, Charter, Section 9.11 S(b), requires Board of Supervisors approval by
Resolution of any contract which, when entered into, extends over 10 years, and of any
contract which, when entered into, costs the City $10,000,000 or more; and
WHEREAS, The proposed amendment contained in , is substantially in
final form, with all material terms and conditions included, and only remains to be executed by
the parties upon approval of this Resolution; now, therefore, be it
RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors hereby approves the amendment in
substantially the form contained in ; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors authorizes DPH to make any
modifications to the amendment, prior to its final execution by all parties, that DPH
determines, in consultation with the City Attorney, are consistent with this Resolution, in the
best interest of the City, do not materially increase the obligations or liabilities of the City, are
necessary or advisable to effectuate the purposes of the amendment, and are in compliance
with all applicable laws, including the City's Charter; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That within 30 days of the amendment being fully executed by
all parties, DPH shall submit to the Clerk of the Board of Supervisors a completely executed
copy for inclusion in ; this requirement and obligation resides with the
Department, and is for purposes of having a complete file only, and in no manner affects the
validity of approved amendment.
