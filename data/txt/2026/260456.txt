Resolution retroactively authorizing the Police Department to accept and expend an
in-kind gift of 1,800 units of Naloxone in 2025 valued at $43,200 through the
Naloxone Distribution Project, which is funded by the Substance Abuse and Mental
Health Services Administration and administered by the Department of Health Care
Services.
WHEREAS, The Police Department applied for a total of 1,800 units of Naloxone
through the Naloxone Distribution Project in 2025, funded by the Substance Abuse and
Mental Health Services Administration and administered by the Department of Health Care
Services to combat opioid overdose-related deaths; and
WHEREAS, The Naloxone Distribution Project accepted the applications and
approved shipment of 600 units of Naloxone each on , , and
, totaling 1,800 units of Naloxone valued at $43,200; now, therefore, be
it
RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors approves the in-kind gift valued at
$43,200 and authorizes the Police Department to retroactively accept the 1,800 units of
Naloxone.
