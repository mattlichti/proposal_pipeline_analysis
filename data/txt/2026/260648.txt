Resolution initiating a landmark designation under Article 10 of the Planning Code for
the Jefferson Airplane House, located at 2400 Fulton Street, Assessor's Parcel Block
No. 1168, Lot No. 007.
WHEREAS, Under Planning Code, Section 1004.1, the Board of Supervisors may by
Resolution initiate landmark designation; and
WHEREAS, Planning Code, Section 1004.2(b), requires the Historic Preservation
Commission to respond to historic district or individual landmark designations initiated by the
1 Board of Supervisors within 90 days, and authorizes the Board, by Resolution, to extend the
time within which the Historic Preservation Commission is to render its decision; and
WHEREAS, The Jefferson Airplane House, located at 2400 Fulton Street, Assessor's
Parcel Block No. 1168, Lot No. 007, served as a band house for Jefferson Airplane, one of the
most quintessential "San Francisco Sound" bands associated with 1960s Counterculture, a
short-lived but internationally influential movement in San Francisco; and
WHEREAS, Jefferson Airplane purchased the property in 1968, and the house served
as the band's creative rehearsal space and recording studio; and
WHEREAS, The property also included space for the band's business office and
served as a residence for some of the members; and
WHEREAS, Jefferson Airplane were the first San Francisco Sound band signed by a
major label, and unique in being female fronted with Grace Slick, who resided at the property,
serving as singer/songwriter; and
WHEREAS, Jefferson Airplane was also known for hosting lavish parties out of the
subject property, and continued to own the property through the mid-1980s; and
Supervisors Chan; Mandelman
WHEREAS, The home is also an exemplary example of Classical Revival architecture,
designed by R.A. Vance of the Vance Lumber Company who imported wood from all over the
world for the house; and
WHEREAS, The home remains intact and has been subject to few exterior alterations
since Jefferson Airplane occupied the space; and
WHEREAS, In 2024, the Historic Preservation Commission adopted the Citywide
Historic Context Statement for Counterculture (1965-1975), which provided frameworks for
identifying and evaluating sites for historical significance and integrity associated with San
Francisco's counterculture history; and
WHEREAS, The Citywide Historic Context Statement for Counterculture (1965-1975)
was prepared by qualified historians in accordance with the Secretary of the Interior's
Standards; and
WHEREAS, Through applying the evaluative framework as outlined in the Citywide
Historic Context Statement for Counterculture (1965-1975), the property can be considered an
individually eligible historic resource based on its strong association with Counterculture
History; now, therefore, be it
RESOLVED, The Board of Supervisors hereby initiates landmark designation of the
Jefferson Airplane House under Planning Code, Section 1004.1; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, The Board requests that the Planning Department prepare a
landmark designation report to submit to the Historic Preservation Commission for its
consideration of the full historical, architectural, aesthetic, and cultural interest and value of
the Jefferson Airplane House; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, The Board of Supervisors requests that the Historic
Preservation Commission consider whether the Jefferson Airplane House warrants landmark
designation and submit its recommendation to the Board according to Article 10 of the
Planning Code.
