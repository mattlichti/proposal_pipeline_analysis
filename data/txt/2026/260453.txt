Resolution retroactively authorizing the Human Services Agency to accept and expend
from the Federal Office of Refugee Resettlement for participation in a program, entitled
"Housing Assistance for Ukrainians (HAU) - San Francisco," a grant increase in the
amount of $300,000 for a total amount of $1,772,856 for the period of 
through .
WHEREAS, The California Department of Social Services (COSS) Refugee Programs
Bureau (RPB), using Federal Fiscal Year (FFY) 2023 Additional Ukraine Supplemental
Appropriations Act (AUSAA) funds provided by the Federal Office of Refugee Resettlement
(ORR), has developed the Housing Assistance for Ukrainians (HAU) Program, to support the
transition of recent Ukrainian newcomers to long-term housing in California; and
WHEREAS, COSS issued a funding revision, dated , notifying the
City and County of San Francisco of its grant award dollar amount of $1,772,856, multi-year
grant period dates of through , and instructions to
adhere to the terms and conditions of HAU grant funds; and
WHEREAS, The Human Services Agency (HSA) intends to utilize these grant funds
towards the provision of direct housing assistance to currently housed eligible Ukrainian
populations and other non-Ukrainian individuals displaced from Ukraine; and
WHEREAS, HSA will not use these funds to supplant existing funds paying for current
services; and
WHEREAS, The grant does not require an ASO amendment; and
WHEREAS, A grant increase of $300,000 from $1,472,856 was approved for the
period of through ; and
WHEREAS, HSA proposes to maximize use of available grant funds on program
expenditures by not including indirect costs in the grant budget; now, therefore, be it
RESOLVED, That HSA is hereby retroactively authorized and directed to accept and
expend the COSS RPB HAU grant award in the amount of $1,772,856 to support the
transition of recent Ukrainian newcomers to long-term housing in San Francisco; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors hereby waives inclusion of
indirect costs in the grant budget; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Executive Director of HSA, or his or her designee, is
hereby authorized and directed to act on behalf of City in connection with the HAU grant
funds, and to enter into, execute, and deliver any and all documents required or deemed
necessary or appropriate to participate in the HAU Program.
