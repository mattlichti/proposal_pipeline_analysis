Resolution authorizing the Department of Public Works to accept and expend a grant in
the amount of up to $14,500,000 from the San Francisco Downtown Development
Corporation, a California nonprofit public benefit corporation, to fund the Powell Street
Improvement Project; approving the associated grant agreement pursuant to Charter,
Section 9.11 S(a), effective upon approval of the Resolution and upon execution of the
grant agreement through ; and affirming the Planning Department's
WHEREAS, Since 2025, the Office of the Mayor, the Office of Economic and
Workforce Development, the Department of Public Works (the "Department"), and other City
departments have discussed, generated, and collaborated on strategies and concepts
intended to revitalize San Francisco neighborhoods including the three-block corridor of
Powell Street between Market and Geary Streets (the "Project Location") for the wellbeing of
San Francisco's diverse community; and
WHEREAS, The City desires to design and complete a streetscape and pedestrian
improvement project at the Project Location that will include pedestrian improvements,
circulation improvements, streetscape surface and landscape enhancements including a
large-scale programmable LED light installation above the cable car turnaround at Market
Street, utility and infrastructure modifications, traffic signal and street lighting modifications,
and cable car stop and loading zone modifications as further described in the Grant
Agreement on file with the Clerk of the Board of Supervisors in and
incorporated herein by reference (the "Powell Street Improvement Project"); and
WHEREAS, On , following a competitive contracting process, the
Public Works Commission adopted , awarding a contract to Clark
Construction Group California, LP, for an amount not to exceed $1,047,819 to perform pre
construction services including cost estimation, constructability reviews, non-intrusive site
investigations, subcontractor pre-qualification, and preliminary design services pertaining to
potential Powell Street Improvement Project concepts consisting of pedestrian and
streetscape improvements, including streetscape renovations, widened sidewalks, and a
programmable LED catenary light installation; and
WHEREAS, On , the Public Works Commission adopted , awarding a contract to Field Operations, LLC for an amount not to exceed
$5,000,000 for professional engineering services in connection with the design of potential
Powell Street Improvement Project concepts; and
WHEREAS, On , the Planning Department prepared and published for
public review the Draft Mitigated Negative Declaration for the San Francisco Better Streets
Plan (Case No. 2007.1238E or "BSP"), which included adoption of a set of citywide
streetscape and pedestrian goals, objectives, policies, and design guidelines, as well as future
strategies to improve the San Francisco pedestrian realm including sidewalks, crosswalks,
and portions of the roadway and the Draft Mitigated Negative Declaration for the BSP was
available for public comment; and
WHEREAS, On , the Planning Department adopted the Final
Mitigated Negative Declaration (the "FMND") pursuant to the California Environmental Quality
Act (California Public Resources Code Sections 21000 et seq.) ("CEQA") and determined
that, with the adoption of the mitigation measures contained in the Mitigation Monitoring and
Reporting Program associated with the BSP ("MMRP"), there is no substantial evidence that
the BSP would have a significant effect on the environment based on Sections 15064
(Determining Significant Effect), 15065 (Mandatory Findings of Significance), and 15070
(Decision to Prepare a Negative Declaration) of the CEQA Guidelines (14 California Code of
Regulations, Sections 15000 et seq.) and the reasons documented in the FMND; and
WHEREAS, On , the Planning Commission, in 
("Planning Commission Resolution"), adopted findings under CEQA, the CEQA Guidelines,
and Chapter 31 of the Administrative Code ("CEQA Findings"), and adopted the MMRP; and
WHEREAS, Ordinance No. 310-10, in which the Board of Supervisors adopted the
CEQA Findings and the BSP, became effective on ; and
WHEREAS, On , the Planning Department issued a determination
("CEQA Determination") concluding that the Project is within the scope of the FMND and, with
the adoption of the mitigation measures contained in the MMRP, no supplemental
environmental review or additional documentation is required; and
WHEREAS, The San Francisco Downtown Development Corporation, a California
nonprofit public benefit corporation (the "DCC"), was founded in 2025 with the purpose of
revitalizing San Francisco, including its downtown area, and ensuring its residents and visitors
have a safe, clean, dynamic, economically vibrant, and innovative place to live, work, play,
and conduct business; and
WHEREAS, The DOC works in close coordination with the City and business, labor,
and civic leaders with the goal of transforming downtown into a thriving destination that drives
economic growth and strengthens San Francisco's cultural fabric; and
WHEREAS, The DOC proposes to provide a grant raised from third-party philanthropic
contributions of up to $14,500,000 (the "Grant") for the Powell Street Improvement Project
according to the terms and conditions set forth in the Grant Agreement; and
WHEREAS, The Department has determined that no indirect costs will be charged to
the Grant; and
WHEREAS, The Board of Supervisors has reviewed and considered the FMND, the
Planning Commission Resolution, the CEQA Findings, and the CEQA Determination, and the
preceding documents are on file with the Clerk of the Board of Supervisors in 
and incorporated herein by reference; now, therefore, be it
RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors has reviewed and considered the FMND
and record as a whole and finds that the FMND is adequate for its use as the decision-making
body for the approval of the Grant Agreement and the Department's acceptance and
expenditure of the Grant for the Powell Street Improvement Project and incorporates the
CEQA Findings set forth in the Planning Commission Resolution by this reference as though
set forth in this Resolution; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors finds that since the FMND was
finalized, there have been no substantial changes to the BSP and no substantial changes in
the circumstances pertaining to the BSP that would require major revisions to the FMND due
to the involvement of new significant environmental effects or an increase in the severity of
previously identified significant impacts, and there is no new information of substantial
importance that would change the conclusions set forth in the FMND; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors finds that the Project would not
cause new significant impacts or a substantial increase in the severity of impacts identified
and analyzed in the FMND, and that the actions approved herein would not necessitate
implementation of additional or considerably different mitigation measures than those
identified in the FMND; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors adopts as its own the Planning
Department's determination in the CEQA Determination that there is no substantial evidence
that the Project could have a significant effect on the environment; and be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors, pursuant to Charter, Section
9.11 S(a), hereby approves the Grant Agreement with the DOC, in an amount of up to
$14,500,000 and authorizes the Director of Public Works or the Director's designee to execute
the Grant Agreement and accept and expend the Grant of up to $14,500,000 for the Powell
Street Improvement Project; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors hereby waives inclusion of
indirect costs in the Grant budget; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Director of Public Works or the Director's designee is
authorized to execute all other documents associated with the Grant Agreement as may be
necessary to implement the intent of this Resolution as determined by the City Attorney; and,
be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That within 30 days of the Grant Agreement being executed
by all parties, Public Works shall provide the final agreement to the Clerk of the Board for
inclusion in .
