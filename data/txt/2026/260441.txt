Resolution approving the Lease Termination Agreement for the Harvey Milk Terminal 1
Food and Beverage Concession Leases in Phases 3 and 4 - Lease 12, Lease
No. 20-0042 between HFF SFO TWO, LLC, as tenant, and the City and County of San
Francisco, acting by and through its Airport Commission.
WHEREAS, On , by , the Airport Commission
awarded the Harvey Milk Terminal 1 Food and Beverage Concession Leases in Phases 3
and 4 - Lease 12, Lease No. 20-0042 (Lease) to HFF SFO TWO, LLC for a term of 12 years
with one two-year option to extend at the Airport's sole discretion, and an initial minimum
annual guarantee of $545,000; and
WHEREAS, On , by Resolution 560-21, the San Francisco Board
of Supervisors approved the Lease; and
WHEREAS, Since the Lease was awarded construction costs are estimated to have
increased 22% and labor costs are estimated to have increased 10-15% rendering the Lease
no longer financially viable; and
WHEREAS, Airport staff believes recapturing the premises will provide the Airport with
an opportunity to re-lease the premises for a higher and better use to better serve the
traveling public; and
WHEREAS, The Airport and Tenant have negotiated a Lease Termination Agreement
which provides for the Tenant to surrender the premises; and
WHEREAS, On , by , the Commission approved
the Lease Termination Agreement; now, therefore, be it
RESOLVED, That this Board of Supervisors hereby approves the Lease Termination
Agreement for the Harvey Milk Terminal 1 Food and Beverage Concession Leases in Phases
3 and 4 - Lease 12, Lease No. 20-0042, a copy of which is on file with the Clerk of the Board
of Supervisors in ; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That within thirty (30) days of this Lease Termination
Agreement being fully executed by all parties, the Airport Commission shall provide the final
agreement to the Clerk of the Board for inclusion into the official file.
