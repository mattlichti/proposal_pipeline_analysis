Resolution authorizing the Sheriff's Office to contract with the San Francisco
Pretrial Diversion Project (SF Pretrial) for Pretrial Services for a three year period
from , through , for a contract total not to exceed amount
of $22,532,145, with two one-year options to extend.
WHEREAS, Charter, Section 9.118, requires that agreements entered into by a
department or commission having a term in excess of ten years or requiring anticipated
expenditures of ten million dollars or more be approved by the Board of Supervisors; and
WHEREAS, The Contract Monitoring Division determined Chapter 14B
subcontracting participation requirement for the Services has been waived pursuant to
waiver CMD14BPREBID0002762; and
WHEREAS, The Sheriff's Office has made it a top priority to provide the San
Francisco Superior Court with options other than detention and cash bail by ensuring a
continuum of pretrial supervision and diversion services with a focus on addressing the
personal barriers that often engender cycles of justice involvement; and
WHEREAS, On , the Sheriff's Office issued a Request for
Proposals for Pretrial Services and as a result of that competitive selection process,
identified the San Francisco Pretrial Diversion Project (SF Pretrial) to receive a contract in
the amount of $22,532,145 over three years to provide Pretrial Services; and
WHEREAS, The City and County of San Francisco has been offering pretrial
services and assessment for nearly 50 years through a partnership between the Superior
Court, Bar Association of San Francisco, Sheriff's Office, District Attorney's Office, Public
Defender's Office, and SF Pretrial; and
WHEREAS, The City and County of San Francisco has utilized a non-profit
provider for pretrial services since 1964, and SF Pretrial has provided components of
these services since 1976 through grant agreements between the Sheriff's Office and SF
Pretrial, thereby falling within the outline of San Francisco Board of Supervisors
, which stated that the operating model for pretrial services "should
reflect the principles of neutrality and structural independence from the law enforcement
functions;" and
WHEREAS, SF Pretrial is a non-profit entity, and thus the proposed contract also
meets the idea supported in Board for use of a non-profit
organization to provide Pretrial Services; and
WHEREAS, Under the proposed contract, SF Pretrial Services will continue its
decades-long experience in working with San Francisco justice involved individuals to
provide a Pretrial Risk Assessment under a staffing model to guarantee
expedient screening and case processing for prospective pretrial clients; and
WHEREAS, The proposed contract will provide for screening of all individuals with
a new misdemeanor or felony charge for pretrial release, which involves the collection
and analysis of case materials, including a completed Public Safety Assessment (PSA)
report, a supplementary criminal history summary, the incident report, and the complete
records of arrest and prosecution (RAP) to be provided to the Court; and
WHEREAS, As of February 2020, the Federal Court enjoined the San Francisco
Sheriff from administering the local bail schedule, and instead requires all cases eligible
for pre-arraignment review to be submitted to a judge within 8 hours of the individual's
identification (ID) confirmation at the county jail, and requires a judge to make a release
determination for that case within 18 hours of ID confirmation; and
WHEREAS, The proposed contract will assist not only in complying with the
Federal Court injunction but will also promote the goal of pretrial release of those eligible
for such release; and
WHEREAS, The proposed contract agreement with SF Pretrial is on file with the Clerk
of the Board of Supervisors in , which is hereby declared to be a part of this
resolution as set forth fully herein; and
WHEREAS, Per the San Francisco Code of Civil Procedure (CCP), Section 1094.5, on
, the Civil Service Commission approved Personal Services Contract (PSC) File
No. DHRPSC0006189 for Pretrial Services; now, therefore, be it
RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors authorizes the Sheriff's Office to execute
the Contract, which is on file with the Clerk of the Board of Supervisors in ;
and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors hereby approves the Pretrial
Services Agreement by and between SF Pretrial and the City and County of San Francisco,
acting by and through its Sheriff's Office, for a three year term beginning , with
two one-year options exercisable by the Sheriff's Office; and, be it
FUTHER RESOLVED, That within thirty (30) days of the Contract being fully executed
by all parties, the Sheriff's Office shall provide the final Contract to the Clerk of the Board for
inclusion in the Board of Supervisors in .
