Resolution approving the second amendment to the grant agreement between the
Tenderloin Housing Clinic, Inc. and the Department of Homelessness and Supportive
Housing ("HSH"), for supportive services, property management, and master leasing
for the Crown, Winton, and National hotels; extending the term by 18 months from , for a total term on , through , and by increasing
the agreement amount by $8,206,214 for a new total amount not to exceed $42,532,462;
and authorizing HSH to enter into any amendments or other modifications to the
Amendment that do not materially increase the obligations or liabilities, or materially
decrease the benefits to the City and are necessary or advisable to effectuate the
purposes of the Agreement.
WHEREAS, The mission of the Department of Homelessness and Supportive Housing
("HSH" or "Department") is to prevent homelessness when possible and make homelessness
rare, brief, and one-time in the City and County of San Francisco ("the City") through the
provision of coordinated, compassionate, and high-quality services; and
WHEREAS, Permanent supportive housing ("PSH") is the most effective evidence
based solution to chronic homelessness; and
WHEREAS, The nonprofit provider Tenderloin Housing Clinic, Inc. ("THC") has
extensive experience operating permanent supportive housing; and
WHEREAS, THC is the longtime provider of supportive services, property
management, and master leasing at the Crown, Winton, and National Hotels and has held
grant agreements with HSH for both locally and federally funded services at these sites since
HSH's founding in 2016; and
WHEREAS, HSH awarded the Agreement to THC through the Department's
streamlined contracting authority for homeless services under Administrative Code,
Chapter 21.B; and
WHEREAS, In July 2021, HSH and THC entered into an Agreement to provide
supportive services, property management, and master leasing for formerly homeless adults
at the Crown, Winton, and National Hotels, ("Original Agreement"); and
WHEREAS, The Original Agreement has a term of , through , and a not to exceed amount of $9,990,000; and
WHEREAS, The Original Agreement is on file with the Clerk of the Board of
Supervisors ("Clerk") in , which is hereby declared to be part of this Resolution
as if set forth fully herein; and
WHEREAS, In May 2023, HSH and THC entered into a First Amendment to continue
these services ("First Amendment"); and
WHEREAS, The First Amendment extended the term by 36 months from , for a total term of , through , and increased the not to exceed
amount by $24,336,248 for a total amount not to exceed of $34,326,248; and
WHEREAS, On , the Board of Supervisors adopted 
23 (), approving the First Amendment; and
WHEREAS, The First Amendment is on file with the Clerk in , which is
hereby declared to be part of this Resolution as if set forth fully herein; and
WHEREAS, HSH intends to enter into a Second Amendment to continue these
services by extending the term by 18 months through , and by increasing
the maximum expenditure by $8,206,214 for a total not to exceed amount of $42,532,462 (the
"Amendment"); and
WHEREAS, The Amendment requires Board of Supervisors approval under Charter,
Section 9.118; now, therefore, be it
RESOLVED, The proposed Amendment contained in , is substantially
in final form, with all materials terms and conditions included, and only remains to be executed
by the parties upon approval of this Resolution; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors authorizes HSH to make any
modifications to the Amendment prior to its final execution by all parties, that HSH determines,
in consultation with the City Attorney, are consistent with this Resolution, in the best interest of
the City, do not materially increase the obligations or liabilities of the City, are necessary or
advisable to effectuate the purposes of the Amendment, and are in compliance with all
applicable laws, including City's Charter; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That within 30 days of the Amendment being fully executed by
all parties, HSH shall submit to the Clerk a completely executed copy for inclusion in ; this requirement and obligation resides with the Department, and is for purposes
of having a complete file only, and in no manner affects the validity of approved Amendment.
