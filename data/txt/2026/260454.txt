Resolution retroactively authorizing the Human Services Agency to accept and expend
a grant from the Federal Office of Refugee Resettlement through the California
Department of Social Services for participation in a program, entitled "Ukrainian
Refugee Support Services Supplemental Funding - San Francisco," for a total amount
of $200,000 for the period of , to .
WHEREAS, The Federal Office of Refugee Resettlement (ORR) awarded the
1 California Department of Social Services (COSS) one-time Federal Fiscal Year (FFY) 2023
Additional Ukraine Supplemental Appropriations Act (AUSAA) funds, to help eligible
populations receive mainstream benefits and provide eligible populations with resettlement
assistance; and
WHEREAS, The California Department of Social Services issued a grant award, dated
, notifying City and County of San Francisco of its grant award dollar
amount of $200,000, multi-year grant period dates of through , and instructions to comply with the eligibility requirements for use of AUSAA funding,
funding reporting and invoicing requirements, and other guidance outlined in Policy Letter 22-
15, posted on , and revised on ; and
WHEREAS, The San Francisco Human Services Agency intends to utilize these grant
funds towards the provision of intensive case management services to eligible populations, to
facilitate connection of eligible populations with public assistance benefits, and refer eligible
populations to services provided by the Human Services Agency and community-based
organizations; and
Mayor Lurie; Supervisors Chan, Dorsey, Mandelman
WHEREAS, The Human Services Agency will not use these funds to supplant existing
funds paying for current services; and
WHEREAS, The grant does not require an ASO amendment; and
WHEREAS, The Human Services Agency proposes to maximize use of available grant
funds on program expenditures by not including indirect costs in the grant budget; now,
therefore, be it
RESOLVED, That the Human Services Agency is hereby retroactively authorized and
directed to accept and expend the Ukrainian Refugee Support Services Supplemental
Funding in the amount of $200,000 to facilitate the connection of persons displaced from
Ukraine with safety net benefits and services; now, therefore, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors hereby waives inclusion on
indirect costs in the grant budget; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Executive Director of the Human Services Agency, or
his or her designee, is hereby authorized and directed to act on behalf of the City in
connection with the Ukrainian Refugee Support Services Supplemental Funding, and to enter
into, execute, and deliver any and all documents required or deemed necessary or
appropriate to accept and expend this Supplemental Funding.
