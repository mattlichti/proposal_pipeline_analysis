Resolution adding the commemorative street name "Tony Stefani Way" to Falmouth
Street between Folsom Street and Shipley Street in recognition of retired San
Francisco Fire Department Captain Tony Stefani.
WHEREAS, Tony Stefani joined the San Francisco Fire Department (SFFD) in 1974,
serving the city for 28 years and spending the majority of his career at Fire Station 1, located
at 935 Folsom Street and directly adjacent to the designated block of Falmouth Street, where
he rose to the rank of Captain of Rescue 1, one of the busiest rescue units in the United
1 States; and
WHEREAS, Captain Stefani testified before the U.S. Senate on the dangers of toxic
flame retardants, contributing to legislative changes and national policy shifts, and his work as
a national leader in firefighter health was featured in the 2013 HBO documentary Toxic Hot
Seat; and
WHEREAS, Driven by a desire to "pay it forward," Captain Stefani founded the San
Francisco Firefighters Cancer Prevention Foundation (SFFCPF) in 2006 to address the
epidemic of cancer among his colleagues; and
WHEREAS, The designation of street signage is commemorative in nature and is not
subject to procedural hearings or noticing pursuant to California Streets and Highway Code,
Sections 970.5 and 5026; California Government Code, Section 5026; and San Francisco
Transportation Code, Section 2.2, for official street names; and
WHEREAS, Commemorative naming of Falmouth Street between Folsom Street and
Shipley Street to "Tony Stefani Way" will serve as a lasting tribute to Captain Stefani's
Mayor Lurie; Supervisors Dorsey, Mandelman, Wong, Chan, Mahmood, Melgar, Chen, Sherrill, Sauter
pioneering work in firefighter occupational health and a daily reminder to the public of his
mission to protect his fellow first responders; now, therefore, be it
RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors of the City and County of San Francisco
hereby honors Captain Tony Stefani by adopting "Tony Stefani Way" as the commemorative
street name for Falmouth Street between Folsom Street and Shipley Street; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors requests that the Municipal
Transportation Agency erect commemorative street signage on Falmouth Street, bounded by
Folsom Street and Shipley Street, indicating both the honorary street name and the existing
official street name, either on a single sign distinguishing the honorary street name from the
official street name or on separate signs, with the honorary name "Tony Stefani Way" placed
beneath the existing official street name; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Department of Public Works is directed to add the
honorary street name to its official list of Commemorative Street Names in accordance with
the Public Works Code, Sections 789 et seq.
