Resolution authorizing the Director of Transportation to enter into a loan agreement
with the Metropolitan Transportation Commission as lender, for San Francisco
Municipal Transportation Agency public transit operating purposes, up to a maximum
principal amount of $200,000,000, for a 12 year term from , through , pursuant to Assembly Bill No. 117.
WHEREAS, The San Francisco Municipal Transportation Agency (SFMTA) is a
1O department of the City and County of San Francisco responsible for the management,
operations, and planning of the City's transportation network; and
WHEREAS, Assembly Bill No. 117 (AB 117), signed by Governor Newsom on
, requires the California State Transportation Agency (CalSTA) to loan $590
million of Transit and Intercity Rail Capital Program (TIRCP) to the Metropolitan
Transportation Commission (MTC) for the purpose of providing emergency transit operating
assistance to eligible Bay Area transit operators; and
WHEREAS, AB 117 identifies the SFMTA as an eligible transit operator and MTC will
loan up to $200,000,000 (MTC Loan) to the SFMTA pursuant to a loan agreement (MTC Loan
Agreement) with terms consistent with the loan agreement between CalSTA and MTC for the
TIRCP funds (CalSTA Loan Agreement); and
WHEREAS, In the absence of the loan, the SFMTA faces a projected operating deficit
for Fiscal Year 2026-27 (beginning ) and the MTC Loan proceeds would provide
critical near-term liquidity to maintain existing service levels for riders; and
WHEREAS, The proposed form of MTC Loan Agreement is on file with the Clerk of the
Board of Supervisors in , is consistent with the form of AB 117 loan
agreement between MTC and CalSTA, and includes the following terms: a maximum principal
amount of up to $200,000,000; a 12-year term maturing on ; a variable interest
rate based on the quarterly rate for the Surplus Money Investment Fund (SMIF) published by
the State Controller; an interest-only period through ; an approximate ten-year
amortization period (, through ) with semiannual principal and
interest payments; repayment secured by the revenue-based portion of State Transit
Assistance Program funds allocable to the SFMTA; an absolute and unconditional repayment
obligation; and proceeds restricted to SFMTA public transit operating purposes; and
WHEREAS, The MTC Loan may not be used for capital expenditures or the retirement
or refinancing of existing indebtedness; and
WHEREAS, Charter, Section 8A.102(b)(13), authorizes the SFMTA to incur debt for its
purposes if the SFMTA Board of Directors recommends that action to the Board of
Supervisors and the Board of Supervisors concurs and authorizes the SFMTA to incur that
debt and other obligations on behalf of the City as long as the City Controller first certifies that
sufficient unencumbered balances are expected to be available in the proper fund to meet all
payments under those obligations as they become due, and any debt obligation, if secured, is
secured by revenues or assets under the jurisdiction of the SFMTA; and
WHEREAS, The MTC Loan will be a secured debt and create obligations for SFMTA
purposes; and
WHEREAS, The SFMTA Board of Directors recommended and requested the Board of
Supervisors to authorize the Director of Transportation to execute the MTC Loan Agreement
in a form approved by the Board of Supervisors and consistent with the CalST A-MTC loan
agreement under AB 117 at its meeting of , by ; and
WHEREAS, By a letter dated , which is on file with the Clerk of the Board
of Supervisors in , the City Controller certified sufficient unencumbered
balances are expected to be available in the proper fund to meet all MTC Loan payments as
they become due, and the MTC Loan is secured by revenues under the jurisdiction of the
SFMTA; and
WHEREAS, The SFMTA, under authority delegated by the Planning Department, has
determined that requesting approval to execute the MTC Loan Agreement is not a "project"
under the California Environmental Quality Act (CEQA) pursuant to Title 14 of the California
Code of Regulations, Sections 15060(c) and 15378(b), with that determination (CEQA
Determination) on file with the Clerk of the Board of Supervisors in ; now,
therefore, be it
RESOLVED, The Board of Supervisors hereby affirms and adopts the SFMTA's
determination under CEQA for the reasons set forth in the CEQA Determination; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, The Board of Supervisors authorizes the Director of
Transportation to enter into the MTC Loan Agreement with the MTC in substantially the form
on file, to enter into any additions, amendments or other modifications to the MTC Loan
Agreement that the Director of Transportation, in consultation with the City Attorney,
determines are in the best interests of the SFMTA and that do not materially increase the
obligations or liabilities of the SFMTA or materially decrease the benefits to the SFMTA as
provided in the MTC Loan Agreement, and to take all actions necessary to implement the
MTC Loan Agreement; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the SFMTA shall provide a copy of the executed MTC
Loan Agreement to the Clerk of the Board of Supervisors within 30 days of the full execution
for inclusion in the office file.
