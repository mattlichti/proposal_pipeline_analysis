Resolution authorizing the Department of Homelessness and Supportive Housing's
Executive Director, Chief Deputies, Deputy Directors and Program Directors to solicit
donations from various private entities and organizations to support the expansion of
temporary shelter and other homeless services to support people experiencing
homelessness, notwithstanding the Behested Payment Ordinance.
WHEREAS, The Mission of the Department of Homelessness and Supportive Housing
("HSH" or "Department") is to prevent homelessness when possible and make homelessness
rare, brief and one-time in the City and County of San Francisco ("the City") through the
provision of coordinated, compassionate and high-quality services; and
WHEREAS, With the enactment of in October 2018, the Board
of Supervisors and Mayor London N. Breed declared a shelter crisis and affirmed San
Francisco's commitment to a continuum of shelter and service options for people experiencing
homelessness; and
WHEREAS, As of the 2026 Point-in-Time Count, there were approximately 7,973
people experiencing homelessness in San Francisco on any given night, 43% of whom were
unsheltered; and
WHEREAS, In April 2023, the City released the five-year strategic plan "Home by the
Bay: An Equity-Driven Plan to Prevent and End Homelessness in San Francisco" which calls
for resources including 1,075 new shelter beds to meet the goals set out in the plan, including
the goal to reduce unsheltered homelessness by 50%; and
WHEREAS, In March 2025, Mayor Daniel Lurie announced his Breaking the Cycle
Initiative framework that is supported by philanthropic and private partnerships to more
effectively get people off the street and connected to services, prevent people from becoming
unhoused, maintain safety and cleanliness in public spaces, and better manage taxpayer
resources; and
WHEREAS, HSH has established successful and innovative public-private partnerships
to expand shelter and other services to meet the critical needs of people experiencing
unsheltered homelessness; and
WHEREAS, The Mayor's Fund for the Homeless (Administrative Code, Section 10.100-
106) was established as a category eight fund to receive all private donations, grants, gifts
and bequests of money, to assist people in need finding long-term housing and overcome
barriers to employment by supporting outreach services, shelters, navigation centers, flexible
housing assistance, housing and other resources; and
WHEREAS, HSH, as the designated administrator of the Mayor's Fund for the
Homeless (Administrative Code, Section 10.100-106) is required to report regularly to the
Homeless Oversight Commission on deposits and expenditures from the fund, as well as
furnish an annual report to the Board of Supervisors showing such gifts received, the nature
or amount of said gifts, and the disposition thereof (Administrative Code, Section 10.100); and
WHEREAS, HSH works with philanthropic organizations, private companies, and
community stakeholders including homeless service providers, often through grant
agreements to support homeless-related initiatives including those under Breaking the Cycle;
and
WHEREAS, On , the Board of Supervisors unanimously approved
 authorizing a waiver of the City's Behested Payment Ordinance
(Campaign and Governmental Conduct Code, Section 3.600 et seq.) for the Office of the
Mayor and HSH, to the extent that is restricts the Mayor, Executive Director of HSH, and
officers and employees of the Office of the Mayor and HSH from soliciting financial support or
in-kind goods or services, from persons or entities that would be considered interested
parties, to support expansion of temporary shelter or other homeless services; and
WHEREAS, On , the Board of Supervisors unanimously approved
 authorizing a six-month waiver of the City's Behested Payment
Ordinance (Campaign and Governmental Conduct Code, Section 3.600 et seq.) for the Office
of the Mayor and HSH that expired on ; and
WHEREAS, On , the Board of Supervisors unanimously approved
 authorizing a six-month waiver of the City's Behested Payment
Ordinance (Campaign and Governmental Conduct Code, Section 3.600 et seq.) for the Office
of the Mayor and HSH that expired on ; and
WHEREAS, On , the Board of Supervisors unanimously approved
Ordinance No. 010-25 authorizing the Office of the Mayor for six months to solicit donations
from various private entities and organizations to support the City's work on the Core
Initiatives, which include Projects Addressing Homelessness, notwithstanding the City's
Behested Payment Ordinance (Campaign and Governmental Conduct Code, Section 3.600 et
seq.); and
WHEREAS, On , the Board of Supervisors approved authorizing a waiver of the City's Behested Payment Ordinance (Campaign and
Governmental Conduct Code, Section 3.600 et seq.) for HSH's Executive Director and
following employees of HSH: Chief Deputies, Deputy Directors, and Program Directors for a
period of six months beginning on ; and
WHEREAS, On , the Board of Supervisors approved authorizing a waiver of the City's Behested Payment Ordinance (Campaign and
Governmental Conduct Code, Section 3.600 et seq.) for HSH's Executive Director and
following employees of HSH: Chief Deputies, Deputy Directors, and Program Directors for a
period of six months beginning ; and
WHEREAS, HSH seeks a new waiver of the City's Behested Payment Ordinance
(Campaign and Governmental Conduct Code, Sections 3.600 et seq.) for the Executive
Director and the following employees of HSH: Chief Deputies, Deputy Directors, and Program
Directors for a period of six months; and
WHEREAS, HSH seek to fundraise or engage in philanthropic partnerships with the
entities that may be considered to be interested parties to the department due to existing
grant agreements with the Department; and
1O WHEREAS, The parties receiving this waiver have agreed to report to the Board of
Supervisors donations to the City and any third party that were solicited under this waiver;
such reports will identify the donor, the amount of the donation, the party that received the
donation, and the interested relationship the donor has with the department that solicited the
donation; the department or office will submit the reports to the Board within 60 days of the
expiration of the six month period authorized by this waiver; now, therefore, be it
RESOLVED, That under Section 3.620(f) of the Campaign and Governmental Conduct
Code, the Board of Supervisors hereby waives application of the City's Behested Payment
Ordinance (Campaign and Governmental Conduct Code, Section 3.600 et seq.) for the
Executive Director and the following employees of HSH: Chief Deputies, Deputy Directors and
Program Directors, so that they may solicit donations from private organizations and
philanthropists to support homelessness-related initiatives including those under Breaking the
Cycle which may include the expansion of temporary shelter and other homeless services to
support people experiencing homelessness for a period of six months, beginning upon the
effective date of this Resolution; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That granting this waiver serves the public interest by
supporting HSH to explore innovative opportunities to expand the availability of resources to
support people experiencing homelessness, specifically temporary shelter and other
homeless services to support bringing more people inside and off the streets; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That granting this waiver does not create an appearance of
impropriety.
