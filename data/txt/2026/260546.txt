Resolution authorizing all City and County of San Francisco Departments to submit
applications for all grants funded by the 2024 Climate Bond (California Proposition 4)
for which they are eligible.
WHEREAS, California voters approved a $10 billion bond in 2024 to fund "projects that
protect our communities from climate change impacts, from wildfire prevention and safe
drinking water to biodiversity protection and expanded access to nature"; and
WHEREAS, The State of California and its agencies, the California Natural Resources
Agency and the California Strategic Growth Council, among others, are responsible for
distributing bond funds through competitive grant opportunities, of which there is over $4
billion available as of May 2026; and
WHEREAS, These agencies' application procedures require, among other things, an
applicant's governing body to declare by Resolution certain authorizations related to the
application and administration of the grant opportunity; and
WHEREAS, Grants that are awarded and fall within the thresholds of Administrative
Code, Section 10.170-1, shall meet all requirements of the Administrative Code before the
grant funds are accepted or expended, including receiving Board of Supervisors' approval, if
required; and
WHEREAS, The application procedures require these final authorizations to be in place
at the time the application is submitted, and a forthcoming round of funding applications is due
; now, therefore, be it
RESOLVED, That all applicable City and County of San Francisco departments,
including, but not limited to, the Department of the Environment, Department of Public Health,
Recreation and Park Department, Office of the City Administrator, are authorized to submit
applications for any and all upcoming California Proposition 4 funding opportunities offered
and for which they are eligible; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That Directors of the applicable City and County departments,
or their designees, are authorized to execute all grant documents and materials necessary to
secure the grant, if awarded; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That this authorization is effective until rescinded by this
Board of Supervisors.
