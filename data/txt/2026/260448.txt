Resolution authorizing adoption of the San Francisco Behavioral Health Services Act
Three-Year Integrated Plan for Fiscal Years (FY) 2026-2029.
WHEREAS, The Behavioral Health Services Act (BHSA) was passed through a ballot
initiative (Proposition 1) in 2024 and provides funding to support county behavioral health
programs; and
WHEREAS, The BHSA specifies three major program components (Full Service
Partnerships, Behavioral Health Services and Supports, and Housing Interventions) for which
funds may be used and the percentage of funds to be devoted to each component; and
WHEREAS, In order to access BHSA funding from the State, counties are required to
1) develop a Three-Year Program and Expenditure Plan (Integrated Plan), and Annual
Updates, in collaboration with stakeholders; 2) post each plan for a 30-day public comment
period; and 3) hold a public hearing on the plan with the County Behavioral Health
Commission; and
WHEREAS, The San Francisco Department of Public Health has submitted and
received approval for a three-year program and expenditure plan (integrated plan) for
FY2023-2026 on file with the Clerk of the Board of Supervisors in ; and
WHEREAS, The San Francisco BHSA Annual Update FY2026-2029, a copy of which
is on file with the Clerk of the Board of Supervisors in , which is hereby
declared to be a part of this Resolution as if set forth fully herein, complies with the BHSA
requirements above, and provides an overview of a three-year plan, various components, and
an expenditure plan for San Francisco for FYs 2026-2029; and
WHEREAS, Welfare and Institutions Code, Section 5847 requires that BHSA Three
Year Integrated Plans, and Annual Updates, be adopted by County Boards of Supervisors
prior to submission to the State; now, therefore, be it
RESOLVED, That the San Francsico BHSA Annual Update for FYs 2026-2029 is
adopted by the Board of Supervisors.
