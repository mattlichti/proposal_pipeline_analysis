Resolution retroactively authorizing the Recreation and Park Department to accept and
expend a grant from the California Natural Resources Agency in the amount of
$1,000,000 for Portsmouth Square Improvement Project; approving the associated
grant agreement for a term limit that is effective upon grant agreement execution
through ; and authorizing the Recreation and Park Department to enter
into amendments or modifications to the agreement that do not materially increase the
obligations or liabilities of the City and are necessary to effectuate the purposes of the
Project or this Resolution.
WHEREAS, The City and County of San Francisco owns Assessor's Parcel Block
No. 0209, Lot No. 017 referred to as Portsmouth Square; and
WHEREAS, Portsmouth Square is one of San Francisco's most significant historic,
cultural, and civic spaces and is under the jurisdiction of the Recreation and Park Department
("RPD"); and
WHEREAS, Through an extensive public planning process, RPD has created a park
design that will facilitate park use by residents of all ages, improve park safety and security,
improve park aesthetics, and acknowledge the site's historical and cultural significance; and
WHEREAS, The California Legislature has delegated responsibility to the California
Natural Resources Agency for the administration of the funding through a General Fund
Specified Grant ("Grant"); and
WHEREAS, On , the San Francisco Recreation and Park Commission
adopted approving RPD's application for Specified Grant funds and
recommended that the Board of Supervisors authorize the Recreation and Park Department
to accept and expend the grant funds for the project and authorize the General Manager to
enter into a grant agreement and negotiate agreements with the State concerning the
administration of the grant; and
WHEREAS, The 2025/2026 California State Budget appropriated funding in the
amount of $1,000,000 for the Grant; and
WHEREAS, As a condition of receiving the Grant, RPO is required to agree to the
terms and conditions of the Grant Agreement which is on file with the Clerk of the Board under
 and which is hereby declared to be part of this Resolution as if set forth fully
herein; and
WHEREAS, The Grant Terms and Conditions prohibit including indirect costs in the
grant budget; and
WHEREAS, The Grant will not require an amendment to the Annual Salary Ordinance;
now, therefore, be it
RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors authorizes the Recreation and Park
Department to accept and expend the Grant; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors hereby waives inclusion of
indirect costs as part of this Grant Budget; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors retroactively approves the Grant
Agreement; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors authorizes the RPO General
Manager to enter into any modifications and amendments to the Grant Agreement including to
any of its exhibits, and authorizes the RPO General Manager to execute further agreements
related to the Grant, that the RPO General Manager determines, in consultation with the City
Attorney, are in the best interests of the City and do not materially increase the obligations orb
it liabilities of the City, are necessary or advisable to effectuate the purposes of the Project or
this Resolution, and are in compliance with all applicable laws, including the City's Charter ..
