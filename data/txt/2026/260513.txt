Resolution authorizing the Department of Public Health to accept a capital gift of a
mobile mammography van valued at $1,500,000 from the San Francisco General
Hospital Foundation (SFGHF) for use by the Zuckerberg San Francisco General
Hospital and Trauma Center (ZSFG), to be purchased with a cash donation of
$1,660,000 from Salesforce, Inc., in consideration for an agreement to name the mobile
mammography van "ZSFG Mammovan - Salesforce in Partnership with ZSFG and
SFGHF for a Healthy Community," for the period of , through .
WHEREAS, San Francisco General Hospital Foundation (SFGHF) has agreed to
donate to the Department of Public Health (DPH) a capital gift of a mobile mammography van
valued at $1,500,000, in support of the Zuckerberg San Francisco General Hospital and
Trauma Center (ZSFG); and
WHEREAS, Salesforce, Inc. gifted funds in the amount of $1,660,000 on , for a new mobile mammography van (Mammovan) to SFGHF; and
WHEREAS, The SFGHF and ZSFG seek to execute a Gift and Naming Agreement that
outlines the responsibilities of the parties for the acceptance, transfer of ownership, and use of
the gift and for the acknowledgement and donor recognition of Salesforce, Inc.; and
WHEREAS, SFGHF intends to "build the vehicle and install all requisite mobile
mammography equipment and related furniture, fixtures and equipment for delivery in 2027"
and to gift the Mammovan to DPH and ZSFG after its completion; and
WHEREAS, The final value of the Mammovan, expected not to exceed $1,500,000.00,
will be disclosed after construction by SFGHF to DPH for purposes of Controller's certification
and asset capitalization, which is a requirement of the City and is not required by SFGHF or
by the donor Salesforce, Inc.; and
WHEREAS, For good and valuable consideration in accordance with the Gift and
Naming Agreement, SFGHF and Salesforce, Inc. would receive naming recognition in the
form of a marketing wrap of the van for the duration of up to fifteen (15) years or for the
operable life of the van, whichever is less, that would identify the name of the van as "ZSFG
Mammovan - Salesforce in Partnership with ZSFG and SFGHF for a Healthy Community";
and
WHEREAS, SFGHF would be solely responsible for placing and maintaining the
marketing wrap, and DPH and ZSFG shall retain approval rights for any designs or
installations of the marketing wrap; now, therefore, be it
RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors approves the capital gift, and authorizes
DPH to accept and expend a capital gift of the mobile mammography van valued in an
amount not to exceed $1,500,000, purchased with the funds donated by Salesforce, Inc. to
SFGHF; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors authorizes DPH to enter into
the Gift and Naming Agreement, naming the mammography van as the "ZSFG Mammovan -
Salesforce in Partnership with ZSFG and SFGHF for a Healthy Community," substantially in
the form of the agreement on file with the Clerk of the Board of Supervisors, in , with such changes or modifications as may be acceptable to the Director of Health
and the City Attorney and which do not materially increase the obligations and liabilities of the
City; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the proceeds of the capital gift by SFGHF will be
accepted and expended consistent with the Administrative Code sections governing the
acceptance of gifts to the City and County of San Francisco, including Administrative Code,
Section 10.100-305; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors extends its gratitude to SFGHF
for the generous capital gift, and to Salesforce, Inc. for the generous underlying donation; and,
be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That within thirty (30) days of the Gift and Naming Agreement
being fully executed by all parties, DPH shall provide a copy of the final agreement to the
Clerk of the Board of Supervisors for inclusion in the official file.
