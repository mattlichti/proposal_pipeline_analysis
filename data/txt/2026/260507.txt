WHEREAS, On , by , the Airport Commission
awarded the Contract No. 50354.01 ("Contract") to SITA Information Networking Computing
USA, Inc. ("SITA") for Airport's Shared Use Passenger Processing Systems in an amount not

WHEREAS, On , by , the Airport Commission
approved Modification No. 1 to the Contract to exercise the three-year option to extend the

WHEREAS, San Francisco Charter, Section 9.11 S(b) provides that the agreements
entered into by a department, board or commission requiring anticipated expenditures by the

Supervisors by Resolution; and
WHEREAS, Copies of Modification No. 1 are on file with the Clerk of the Board of
Supervisors in ; now, therefore, be it
RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors hereby approves Modification No. 1 to
Contract No. 50354.01 with SITA Information Networking Computing USA, Inc. to exercise the
three-year option to extend the term to , and increase the contract amount by
$9,700,546 for a new total not-to-exceed amount of $16,780,546; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That within thirty (30) days of Modification No. 1 being fully
