Resolution approving and authorizing the Director of Real Estate, on behalf of the
following City Departments: San Francisco Municipal Transportation Agency, Police
Accountability, Human Resources, Office of the City Administrator, Mayor's Office of
Housing and Community Development, Office of Community Investment and
Infrastructure, Office of Economic and Workforce Development, Technology, Public
Works, Civil Service Commission, Human Rights Commission, and City Attorney, to
execute a Second Amendment to the lease agreement with Hudson 1455 Market Street,
LLC, as Landlord, to expand the premises to include an additional 502,082 rentable
square feet of office space at 1455 Market Street, for an initial term of 21 years, and
terminating on , with two five-year options to extend the lease term with
an annual base rent of $14,487,462 (year two), and resetting the rent to $40.00 per
square foot beginning , with 1% annual increases until year four and
then 3% annually thereafter; to expend an amount not to exceed $10,760,600 for digital
technology costs, tenant improvements and furniture, fixtures and equipment to the
extent of available operating budget and to the extent not covered by the tenant
improvement allowance; affirming the Planning Department's determination under the
California Environmental Quality Act; adopting the Planning Department's findings of
consistency with the General Plan, and the eight priority policies of the Planning Code,
Section 101.1; and authorizing the Director of Real Estate to execute any amendments,
options to extend the lease term, make certain modifications and take certain actions
that do not materially increase the obligations or liabilities to the City, do not materially
decrease the benefits to the City and are necessary or advisable to effectuate the
purposes of the Second Amendment or this Resolution.
WHEREAS, The City Administrator directed the Real Estate Division ("RED") to
develop a strategic Civic Center space plan that supports long-term operational needs,
improves efficiency through consolidation of City offices, and identifies competitively priced
leasing opportunities to reduce overall occupancy costs; and
WHEREAS, Under the leadership and direction of the City Administrator, RED
identified 1455 Market Street (the "Property"), a 22-story property totaling 1,123,876 rentable
square feet (RSF) (the "Building") as an opportunity for the City to meet both its long-term goal
of consolidating assets through long-term leasing or purchase and short-term cost savings
goals while consolidating Civic Center operations into a modern, resilient building with large
floor plates and favorable market-driven lease terms; and
WHEREAS, On , the Board of Supervisors by 
authorized the Director of Real Estate to execute an initial Lease with Hudson 1455 Market
Street, LLC, a Delaware limited liability company, on behalf of multiple City departments
("Lease") for approximately 157, 154 RSF within the Building ("Phase 1 Premises") for an initial
term of 21 years, terminating on , with two five-year options to extend the term,
an option to lease additional premises ("Lease Option") and a purchase option ("Purchase
Option"); and
WHEREAS, The Lease provides the City with an Additional Premises Option, in which
the City has the right on one or more occasions to lease any additional or vacant full floor
office space in the Building on the then same terms and conditions, provided that the City may
exercise the Additional Premises Option by providing notice to the Landlord on or before
; and
WHEREAS, On , the Director of Real Estate on behalf of the City
provided an Additional Premises Notice for a second phase, adding 232,162 RSF to the
Premises (the "Phase 2 Premises") to be occupied by the Human Services Agency and San
Francisco Public Library - IT; and
WHEREAS, On , the Board of Supervisors by 
authorized the Director of Real Estate to execute the First Amendment to the Lease for the
"Phase 2 Premises" to be occupied by the Human Services Agency and San Francisco Public
Library - IT; and
WHEREAS, The City Administrator has directed the Real Estate Division to evaluate
the City's Civic Center real estate portfolio with the goals of reducing reliance on costly and
inefficient facilities and leveraging favorable market conditions to improve long-term
operational efficiency; and
WHEREAS, The Real Estate Division has identified three Civic Center area assets that
present significant operational, financial, or facility condition challenges to continued City use;
WHEREAS, One South Van Ness Avenue, a City-owned facility, is overcrowded,
operationally inefficient, and contains major building systems at or near end-of-life, resulting in
significant deferred capital needs and making continued occupancy cost-prohibitive; and
WHEREAS, A partial exit of 25 Van Ness Avenue provides for a consolidation of
administrative functions into an appropriately sized footprint that meets business needs, while
enabling essential building improvements that are more feasible to complete when the facility
is not fully occupied; and
WHEREAS, 1390 Market Street, a leased property currently providing office space for
a portion of the City Attorney's Office, is operating at above-market rental rates under a lease
that expires in April 2028; and
WHEREAS, Relocating City departments from these three locations to 1455 Market
Street will reduce reliance on aging Civic Center facilities that require significant capital
investment, avoid substantial deferred maintenance costs, improve operational efficiency
through consolidation into a modern, resilient building, and allow the City to take advantage of
current favorable market conditions by exiting above-market leased space at 1390 Market
Street; and
WHEREAS, On the Director of Real Estate on behalf of the City
provided an Additional Premises Notice for a third phase of the Lease, adding 502,082 RSF to
the Premises to be occupied by the San Francisco Municipal Transportation Agency,
Department of Police Accountability, Department of Human Resources, Office of the City
Administrator, including the divisions of 311, DataSF, Digital Services, the Committee on
Information Technology, Real Estate and Risk Management, Mayor's Office of Housing and
Community Development, Office of Community Investment and Infrastructure, Office of
Economic and Workforce Development, Department of Technology, Department of Public
Works, Civil Service Commission, and City Attorney (together, the "Phase 3 Premises"); and
WHEREAS, The proposed third phase will facilitate the consolidation of departments
currently located within the Phase 1 Premises, including Human Resources and the San
Francisco Municipal Transportation Agency, thereby freeing space presently allocated to
Human Resources and allowing the Human Rights Commission to occupy that space upon
completion of the consolidation;
WHEREAS, RED negotiated additional material economic benefits to the City under
the proposed Second Amendment to the Lease; and
WHEREAS, Under the Second Amendment, the Base Rent amount for all City-
occupied premises under the Lease will be reset to $40 per square foot ("PSF) starting
 ("Reset Date"), resulting in reduced rent obligations compared to the
scheduled rates for the Phase 1 and Phase 2 Premises; and
WHEREAS, Beginning in 2028, Base Rent escalations will be reduced to 1% annually
until the fifth anniversary of the Reset Date and will increase at 3% annually thereafter; and
WHEREAS, Under the Second Amendment, the Base Year for operating expenses will
be adjusted from 2027 to 2028, delaying the commencement of operating expense
pass-throughs and generating annual operating cost savings for the City; and
WHEREAS, The Second Amendment also establishes a Base Year reset every ten
years, with the next reset occurring in 2038, providing improved long-term cost predictability;
WHEREAS, Under the Second Amendment, the City negotiated additional
tenant-beneficial provisions, including caps on controllable operating expenses, three years of
property tax protection, installation of additional electric-vehicle chargers at no cost to the City,
continued rights to parking, and a landlord-provided shared conference room facility; and
WHEREAS, Under the Second Amendment, the Landlord will increase the tenant
improvement allowance from $100 PSF to $180 PSF for a total of approximately $90,374,760
("Tenant Improvement Allowance") and a moving allowance in the amount of $15 PSF for a
total of approximately $7,531,230 ("Moving Allowance" and collectively with the Tenant
Improvement Allowance the "Allowances"), the unused balance of the Moving Allowance can
be applied to tenant improvements, and Landlord will also perform, at its cost, capital
improvements to the Building which will benefit the City as tenant; and
WHEREAS, The City's estimated tenant improvement and technology costs
contribution will be $10,760,600; and
WHEREAS, Under the Second Amendment, the expiration date of the Lease will be
extended from , to ; and
WHEREAS, The Second Amendment, if approved, will add a total of approximately
502,082 rentable square feet of additional Phase 3 Premises to the Lease, for final space
allocations for individual departments will be determined during a City-approved design and
programming process following approval of the Second Amendment; and
WHEREAS, The Landlord estimates that the tenant improvements for the Phase 3
Premises will be completed and ready for occupancy during the first half of Fiscal Year 2027-
2028, with the exception of the City Attorney's office space, which is anticipated to be
delivered by May 2028, thereby allowing the City to coordinate a timely and seamless
relocation from its above-market lease at 1390 Market Street; and
WHEREAS, Under the Purchase Option the City has the right, but not the obligation, to
purchase the Property if the City has leased a total of 400,000 RSF within the Building; and
WHEREAS, With the addition of the Additional Premises, the City will occupy
approximately 930,292 RSF in the Building and be eligible to exercise the Purchase Option in
its sole and absolute discretion; and
WHEREAS, Under the Lease, the City has until , to timely give notice of
its intent to exercise the Purchase Option and until , to close escrow on
the purchase; and
WHEREAS, Under the Second Amendment to the Lease, if the Purchase Option
expires, the City will have a Right of First Offer to Purchase throughout the initial term of the
Lease; and
WHEREAS, The City's exercise of the Lease Option, Purchase Option or Right of First
Offer to Purchase requires separate Board of Supervisors and Mayoral approval in their
respective sole and absolute discretion; and
WHEREAS, RED determined that the Second Amendment provides short-term and
long-term monetary savings and best meets the long-term strategic goals of the City and is in
the best interest of the City; and
WHEREAS, The Planning Department, through an email dated ,
determined that no General Plan Referral was required; a copy of the Planning Department's
email is on file with the Clerk of the Board of Supervisors in ; and
WHEREAS, The Director of Property has determined that the base rent is below fair
market value for similar properties and has confirmed this through an independent appraisal
under Chapter 23; now, therefore, be it
RESOLVED, That in accordance with the recommendation of the Director of Real
Estate, the Board of Supervisors approves the Second Amendment and authorizes the
Director of Real Estate to take all actions necessary to enter into and perform the City's
1O obligations under the Second Amendment; and, be it
Estate to the extent of available operating budget and to the extent not covered by the Tenant
Improvement Allowance to expend an amount not to exceed $10,760,600 for digital
technology costs, tenant improvements and furniture, fixtures and equipment; and be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That the Board of Supervisors hereby affirms the Planning
Department's determination under the California Environmental Quality Act, and adopting the
Planning Department's findings of consistency with the General Plan, and the eight priority
policies of the Planning Code, Section 101.1; and, be it
Estate to execute any amendments, options to extend the Lease term, make certain
modifications and take certain actions that do not materially increase the obligations or
liabilities to the City, do not materially decrease the benefits to the City and are necessary or
advisable to effectuate the purposes of the Second Amendment or this Resolution and are in
compliance with all applicable laws; and, be it
FURTHER RESOLVED, That within 30 days of the execution of the Second
Amendment, the Director of Real Estate shall provide a copy to the Clerk of the Board for the
Board's file.
